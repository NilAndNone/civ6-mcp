## Summary

Dramatic Ages is the fourth game mode in Civilization VI, introduced in the Byzantium & Gaul Pack. It is focused on the Ages mechanic and Loyalty, making the game much more dynamic. It requires either Rise and Fall or Gathering Storm to play.
