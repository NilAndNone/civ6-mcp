## Summary

Bolshoi Theatre
The Bolshoi Theatre is an Industrial Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on flat land adjacent to a Theater Square.
* Effects:
** +2 Writer points per turn
** +2 Musician points per turn
** +1 Great Work Writing Great Work of Writing slot
** +1 Music Icon Great Work of Music slot
** Awards 2 randomly-chosen free civics when completed.
