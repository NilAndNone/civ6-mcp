## Summary

Mausoleum at Halicarnassus
The Mausoleum at Halicarnassus is a Classical Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built adjacent to a Harbor.
* Effects:
** +1 Science, +1 Faith, and +1 Culture on all Coast tiles in this city.
** All Engineers have an additional charge.
