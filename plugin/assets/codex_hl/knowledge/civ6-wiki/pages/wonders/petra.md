## Summary

Petra
Petra is a Classical Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on Desert or Desert Floodplains.
* Effects:
** +2 Food, +2 Gold, and +1 Production on all Desert tiles for this city. (Does not apply to Floodplains.)
