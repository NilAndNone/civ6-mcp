## Summary

Library of Alexandria
The Great Library is a Classical Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on flat land adjacent to a Campus with a Library.
* Effects:
** +2 Science
** +1 Scientist point per turn
** +2 Writing Great Works of Writing slots
** Receive boosts to all Ancient and Classical era technologies.
** +1 Writer point per turn
** Receive a random tech boost after another player recruits a Great Scientist.
