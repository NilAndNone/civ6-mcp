## Summary

Temple of Artemis
The Temple of Artemis is an Ancient Era Wonder in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It must be built adjacent to a Camp.
* Effects:
** +4 Food
** +3 Housing
** Each Camp, Pasture, and Plantation improvement within 4 tiles of this wonder provides +1 Amenity.
