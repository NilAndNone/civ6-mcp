## Summary

Statue of Liberty
The Statue of Liberty is an Industrial Era Wonder in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It must be built on Coast adjacent to land and a Harbor. It cannot be built on a Lake.

## Key Facts

- Era: Industrial
- Introduced in: Rise and Fall
- Cost: 1240
- reqtech: Civil Engineering
- effect: All your cities within 6 tiles are always 100% Loyal.;
 Grants 2 Settlers.;
