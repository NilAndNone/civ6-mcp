## Summary

Great Bath, Mohenjo-daro
The Great Bath is an Ancient Era wonder in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It must be built on Floodplains.
* Effects:
** +3 Housing.
** +1 Amenity from entertainment.
** Floodplains tiles along the river containing this wonder are now immune to Flood damage.
** -50% Production and Food yields from Flood damage.
** +1 Faith to the yields of a tile belonging to this city for every time it has been Flooded.
