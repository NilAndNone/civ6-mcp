## Summary

The Aztec Civilization Pack is DLC for Civilization VI that was released on October 21, 2016, simultaneously with the initial game release. The pack includes a new Aztec civilization which is led by Montezuma.
The DLC was initially available only for players that have preordered the game. The DLC was granted for free to every player 3 months after the release.
