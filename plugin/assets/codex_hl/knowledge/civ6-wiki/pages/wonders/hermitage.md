## Summary

Hermitage Museum
The Hermitage is an Industrial Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built along a River on a non-Desert and non-Tundra tile.
* Effects:
** +3 Artist points per turn
** +4 Landscape Icon Great Works of Art slots
