## Summary

Egyptian pyramids
The Pyramids are an Ancient Era wonder in Civilization VI. They must be built on Desert or Desert Floodplains.
* Effects:
** +2 Culture
** Grants a free Builder
** All Builders receive an extra Build Charges Icon build charge (Applies to both existing Builders and newly trained or purchased ones.)
