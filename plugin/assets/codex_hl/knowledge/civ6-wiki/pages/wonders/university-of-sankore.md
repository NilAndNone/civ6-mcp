## Summary

Sankoré Madrasah
The University of Sankore is a Medieval Era wonder in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It must be built on Desert or Desert Hills next to a Campus with a University.
* Effects:
** +3 Science
** +1 Faith
** +2 Scientist points per turn
** Other civilizations' Trade Routes to this city provide +1 Science and +1 Gold for them
** +2 Science for every Trade Route to this city
** Domestic Trade Routes give an additional +1 Faith to this city
