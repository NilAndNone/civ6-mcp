## Summary

Stonehenge
Stonehenge is an Ancient Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on flat land adjacent to Stone.
* Effects:
** +2 Faith
** Grants a free Prophet (or a free Apostle if no Prophets are available).
** Prophets may found a religion on Stonehenge instead of a Holy Site.
