## Summary

Venetian Arsenal
The Venetian Arsenal is a Renaissance Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on Coast adjacent to an Industrial Zone. It cannot be built on a Lake.
* Effects:
** +2 Engineer points per turn
** Receive a second naval unit for each naval unit trained in one of your cities. (Does not apply when purchasing units.)
