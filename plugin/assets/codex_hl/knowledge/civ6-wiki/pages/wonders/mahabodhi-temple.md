## Summary

Mahabodhi Temple
The Mahabodhi Temple is a Classical Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on Woods adjacent to a Holy Site with a Temple, and you must have founded a Religion.
* Effects:
** +4 Faith
** Grants 2 Apostles.
** +2 Diplomatic Victory Points when built.
