## Summary

Saint Basil's Cathedral
St. Basil's Cathedral is a Renaissance Era Wonder in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It must be built adjacent to a City Center.
* Effects:
** +3 Relic Relic slots
** +100% Tourism Icon Religious Tourism from this city.
** +1 Food, +1 Production, and +1 Culture on all Tundra tiles for this city.
