## Summary

Terracotta Army
The Terracotta Army is a Classical Era wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on Grassland or Plains adjacent to an Encampment with a Barracks or Stable.
* Effects:
** +2 General points per turn
** All current units gain a Promotion level.
** All Archaeologists from the owner may enter foreign lands without Open Borders.
