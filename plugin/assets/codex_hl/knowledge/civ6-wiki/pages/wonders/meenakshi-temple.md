## Summary

Meenakshi Temple
The Meenakshi Temple is a Medieval Era Wonder in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It must be built adjacent to a Holy Site by a player who has founded a religion.
* Effects:
** +3 Faith.
** +2 Gurus.
** Gurus are 30% cheaper to purchase.
** Religious units adjacent to Gurus receive +5 Religious Strength Religious Strength in Theological Combat and +1 Movement.
