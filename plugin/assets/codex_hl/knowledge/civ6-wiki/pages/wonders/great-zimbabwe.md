## Summary

Great Zimbabwe
Great Zimbabwe is a Renaissance Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built adjacent to Cattle and a Commercial Hub with a Market.
* Effects:
** +1 Trade Route
** +5 Gold
** +2 Merchant points per turn
** Trade Routes from this city get +2 Gold for every bonus resource within 3 tiles of the city and in this city's territory
