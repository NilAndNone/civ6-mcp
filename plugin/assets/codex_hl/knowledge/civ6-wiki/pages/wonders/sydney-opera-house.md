## Summary

Sydney Opera House
The Sydney Opera House is an Atomic Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on Coast adjacent to land and a Harbor. It cannot be built on a Lake.
* Effects:
** +8 Culture
** +5 Musician points per turn
** +3 Music Great Works of Music slots
