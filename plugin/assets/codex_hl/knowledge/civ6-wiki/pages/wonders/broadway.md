## Summary

Broadway (Manhattan)
Broadway is a Modern Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on flat land adjacent to a Theater Square.
* Effects:
** +3 Writer points per turn
** +3 Musician points per turn
** +1 Writing Great Works of Writing slot
** +2 Music Great Works of Music slots
** +20% Culture in this city.
** 1 free random Atomic era civic boost.
