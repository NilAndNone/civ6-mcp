## Summary

Angkor Wat
Angkor Wat is a Medieval Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built adjacent to an Aqueduct.
* Effects:
** +2 Faith
** +1 Citizen Population in all current cities when built.
** +1 Housing in all cities.
