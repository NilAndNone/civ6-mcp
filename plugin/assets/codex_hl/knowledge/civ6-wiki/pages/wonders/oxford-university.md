## Summary

University of Oxford
Oxford University is an Industrial Era Wonder in Civilization VI. It must be built on Grasslands or Plains adjacent to a Campus with a University.
* Effects:
** +3 Scientist points per turn
** +2 Writing Great Works of Writing slots
** +20% Science in this city
** Awards 2 randomly-chosen free technologies when completed.
