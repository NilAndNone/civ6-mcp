## Summary

Desert is a base terrain in Civilization VI. It is found in many parts of the world, sometimes as great expanses, sometimes as single tiles.
* Base yields:
** None
* +1 Faith adjacency bonus for Holy Sites with Desert Folklore pantheon
* May cause the formation of dust storms, which will bring destruction but also may fertilize affected tiles, giving them Food and/or Production base yield.
