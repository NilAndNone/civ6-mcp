## Summary

Mount Roraima
Mount Roraima is a four-tile impassable Natural Wonder in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. Mount Roraima appears as a Mountain, and may be found on Grasslands. Each Wonder tile provides +1 Faith, +1 Science, and +2 Appeal to adjacent tiles.
Discovering Mount Roraima grants +1 Era Score, or +3 Score if the player is the first to do so.
