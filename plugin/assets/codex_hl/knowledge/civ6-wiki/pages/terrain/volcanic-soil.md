## Summary

Volcanic Soil is a terrain feature in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It is found on land tiles near Volcanoes that have erupted.
* Yields:
** May get additional Food, Production, Science, and Culture with volcanic eruptions.
* Additional traits:
** +2 Faith with Fire Goddess pantheon
** May receive both the Farm and the Mine improvements
