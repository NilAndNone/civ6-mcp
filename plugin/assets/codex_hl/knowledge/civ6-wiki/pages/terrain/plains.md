## Summary

Plains is a base terrain in Civilization VI. It is found throughout the world.
* Base yields:
** 1 Food, 1 Production
* Can build Farms on flat land (and also on Hills after researching Civil Engineering)
* May cause the formation of tornadoes, which may devastate improvements and Districts and kill units and Citizen Population of nearby cities.
