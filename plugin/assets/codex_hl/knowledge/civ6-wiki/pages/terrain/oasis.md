## Summary

An Oasis is a terrain feature in Civilization VI. It is found on flat inland Desert tiles.
* Yields:
** +3 Food, +1 Gold
* Movement needed:
** 1 Moves Icon MP. It is considered a land tile.
* Additional traits:
** Provides fresh water to adjacent tiles. Can provide fresh water via an Aqueduct.
** +1 Appeal
** +1 Production with Lady of the Reeds and Marshes pantheon ( +2 Production)
** Cannot be developed with tile improvements, Districts, or wonders. Cities may not be founded on Oasis tiles.
