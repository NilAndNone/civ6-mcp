## Summary

Reyna, otherwise known as The Financier, is one of the Governors introduced in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. Her main expertise is increasing Gold income for the city, as well as promoting Tourism in Rise and Fall and generating clean Power in Gathering Storm.
