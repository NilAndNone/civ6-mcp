## Summary

Desert (Hills) is a base terrain in Civilization VI which is a variant of other Desert tiles. Like other Desert tiles, they are usually found in equatorial regions.
Desert Hills provide 1 Production. Desert Hills may be improved with Mines, which makes them vastly more productive than other Desert tiles. All Hills have a +3 Strength Combat Strength modifier, which can strengthen city defenses, as well as protect vulnerable units.
