## Summary

Snow (Hills) is a base terrain, which is a variant of Snow tiles in Civilization VI. Like other Snow tiles, they are usually found at the poles of the map.
Snow Hills provide 1 Production. Snow Hills may be improved with Mines, which makes them vastly more productive than other Snow tiles. All Hills have a +3 Strength Combat Strength modifier, which can strengthen city defenses, as well as protect vulnerable units.
