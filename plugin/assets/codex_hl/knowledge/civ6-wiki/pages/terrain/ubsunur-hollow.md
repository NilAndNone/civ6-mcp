## Summary

Uvs Lake Basin
Ubsunur Hollow is a four-tile passable Natural Wonder in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. Ubsunur Hollow appears as a Marsh on Tundra tiles. Each Wonder tile provides 2 Faith, 1 Food, and 1 Production. Ubsunur Hollow increases the Appeal of adjacent tiles by +2.
Like normal Marsh tiles, Ubsunur Hollow tiles have a Movement cost of 2, and reduce the Strength Combat Strength of defending units by -2.
Discovering Ubsunur Hollow grants +1 Era Score, or +3 Score if the player is the first to do so.
