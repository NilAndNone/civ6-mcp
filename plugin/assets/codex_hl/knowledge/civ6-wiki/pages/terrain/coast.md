## Summary

Coast is a base terrain in Civilization VI. It is found on the continental shelves and everywhere where land meets water, extending up to 3 tiles away from land.
* Base yields:
** 1 Food, 1 Gold
* Allows construction of Harbor and Water Park Districts.
* Allows sea travel before researching Cartography
* Provides +1 Appeal to adjacent tiles.
