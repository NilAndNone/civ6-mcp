## Summary

Ice is a terrain feature in Civilization VI. It is found on Coast and Ocean tiles near the extreme north and south boundaries of the map.
* Yields:
** Nothing, cannot be worked
* Movement needed:
** Impassable
* Additional features:
** Can be removed from tiles by climate change.
Ice is (mostly) an unusable terrain. It contains no resources or other features of any kind. Districts and wonders cannot be placed on Ice tiles.
