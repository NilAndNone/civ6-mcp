## Summary

Tundra is a type of base terrain in Civilization VI. It is found in great expanses everywhere near the polar regions (far north and south) of the world.
* Base yields:
** 1 Food
* May cause the formation of Blizzards, which will bring destruction on improvements and Districts, may kill Citizen Population in nearby cities, but may also fertilize tiles, giving them additional Food and/or Production base yields
* +1 Faith adjacency bonus for Holy Sites with Dance of the Aurora pantheon
