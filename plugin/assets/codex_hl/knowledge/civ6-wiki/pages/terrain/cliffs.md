## Summary

Cliffs are a terrain feature in Civilization VI. They are found on Hills adjacent to Coast tiles.
* Yields:
** Nothing
* Movement needed:
** Blocks embarking/disembarking from this tile

## Key Facts

- found on: Hills, Coast
- notes: * Prevents land units from embarking/disembarking.
* Modifies only hills adjacent to water.
