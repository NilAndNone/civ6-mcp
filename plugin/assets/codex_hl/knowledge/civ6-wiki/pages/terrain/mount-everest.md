## Summary

Mount Everest
Mount Everest is a three-tile impassable natural wonder in Civilization VI. Mount Everest appears as a Mountain, and may be found on Desert, Grassland, Plains, and Tundra tiles (often surrounded by regular Mountains). Each wonder tile provides +1 Faith and +2 Appeal to adjacent tiles.
A religious unit that moves adjacent to Mount Everest receives the Altitude Training ability, which allows it to traverse Hills without Moves penalties.
In Civilization VI: Rise and Fall, discovering Mount Everest grants +1 Era Score, or +3 Score if the player is the first to do so.
