## Summary

GermanyHoly Roman Empire
The German people represent a civilization in Civilization VI. They are led by Frederick Barbarossa, under whom their default colors are gray and black; and Ludwig II, under whom their colors are light blue and white.
The Germans' civilization ability is Free Imperial Cities, which allows their cities to build one more District beyond normal Citizen Population limits. Their unique unit is the U-Boat (which replaces the Submarine), and their unique District is the Hansa (which replaces the Industrial Zone).
