## Summary

Greece
The Greek people represent a civilization in Civilization VI. They are led by Pericles, under whom their colors are light blue and white; and Gorgo, under whom their colors are dark red and light blue.
The Greeks' civilization ability is Plato's Republic, which grants an additional Wildcard Slot Wildcard policy slot in any government. Their unique unit is the Hoplite (which replaces the Spearman), and their unique District is the Acropolis (which replaces the Theater Square).
