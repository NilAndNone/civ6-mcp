## Summary

Zulu Kingdom
The Zulu people represent a civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. They are led by Shaka, under whom their default colors are brown and white.
The Zulus' civilization ability is Isibongo, which causes garrisoned units to increase the Loyalty of their cities and upgrades units into Corps or Armies (and Fleets and Armadas for naval units) when they capture cities (if the necessary civics have been discovered). Their unique unit is the Impi (which replaces the Pikeman), and their unique District is the Ikanda (which replaces the Encampment).
