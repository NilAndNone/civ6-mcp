## Summary

Mongol Empire
The Mongolian people (or Mongols) represent a civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. They are led by Genghis Khan, under whom their default colors are dark red and orange; and (with New Frontier Pass) by Kublai Khan, under whom their default colors are reversed.
The Mongols' civilization ability is Örtöö, which causes their Trade Routes to instantly create Trading Posts in the destination city, as well as an extra level of Visibility Diplomatic Visibility for having a Trading Post in another civilization's city and an additional +3 Strength Combat Strength for each level of Visibility Diplomatic Visibility they have on an opponent. Their unique unit is the Keshig, and their unique building is the Ordu (which replaces the Stable).
