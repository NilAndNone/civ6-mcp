## Summary

Georgia (country)
The Georgian people represent a civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. They are led by Tamar, under whom their default colors are white and orange.
The Georgians' civilization ability is Strength in Unity, which allows them to receive Era Score bonuses for the Dedications they make at the beginning of Golden and Heroic Ages, and grants them extra Production towards walls. Their unique unit is the Khevsur (which replaces the Man-At-Arms), and their unique building is the Tsikhe (which replaces the Renaissance Walls).
In the Dramatic Ages game mode, the Georgians' civilization ability functions differently. They still receive their Production bonus toward walls, but in a Golden Age, they receive an additional Wildcard Slot Icon Wildcard slot in their government and can use Dark Age policy cards.
