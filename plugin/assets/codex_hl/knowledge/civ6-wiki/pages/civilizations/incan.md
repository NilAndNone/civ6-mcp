## Summary

Inca Empire
The Incan people (or Inca(s)) represent a civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. They are led by Pachacuti, under whom their default colors are brown and yellow.
The Incas' civilization ability is Mit'a, which enables their Citizen Citizens to work Mountain tiles (each of which provides +2 Production (increasing to +3 Production when they reach the Industrial Era), and +1 Food for every adjacent Terrace Farm). Their unique unit is the Warak'aq (which replaces the Skirmisher), and their unique tile improvement is the Terrace Farm.
