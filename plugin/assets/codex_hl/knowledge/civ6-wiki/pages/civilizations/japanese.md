## Summary

Japan
The Japanese people represent a civilization in Civilization VI. They are led by Hojo Tokimune, under whom their default colors are white and dark red; and Tokugawa, under whom their default colors are reversed.
The Japanese civilization ability is Meiji Restoration, which allows District to receive a standard adjacency bonus from being next to one another, instead of minor. Their unique unit is the Samurai (which replaces the Man-At-Arms), and their unique building is the Electronics Factory (which replaces the Factory).
