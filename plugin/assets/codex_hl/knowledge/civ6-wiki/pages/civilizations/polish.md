## Summary

Poland
The Polish people (or Poles) represent a civilization in Civilization VI. They are led by Jadwiga, under whom their default colors are dark red and pink. They are available with the Poland Civilization & Scenario Pack, which was released on December 20, 2016.
The Poles' civilization ability is Golden Liberty, which allows them to claim all surrounding tiles when they build an Encampment or a Fort in their territory and changes one of their government's Military Policy Military policy slots into a Wildcard Slot Icon Wildcard slot. Their unique unit is the Winged Hussar (which replaces the Cuirassier), and their unique building is the Sukiennice (which replaces the Market).
