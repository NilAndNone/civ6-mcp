## Summary

France
The French people represent a civilization in Civilization VI. They are led by Black Queen Catherine de Medici, under whom their default colors are blue and light yellow; Magnificence Catherine de Medici, under whom their default colors are white and olive; and Eleanor of Aquitaine (with Gathering Storm), under whom their default colors are pink and light yellow.
The French civilization ability is Grand Tour, which gives them +20% Production towards Medieval, Renaissance, and Industrial era wonders and doubles Tourism from wonders of any era. Their unique unit is the Garde Impériale (which replaces the Line Infantry), and their unique tile improvement is the Château.
