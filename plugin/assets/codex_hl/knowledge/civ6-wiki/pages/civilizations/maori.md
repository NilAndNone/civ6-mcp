## Summary

Māori people
The Māori people represent a civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. They are led by Kupe, under whom their default colors are red and cyan.
The Māori's civilization ability is Mana, which allows them to begin the game with the Sailing and Shipbuilding technologies and the ability to enter Ocean tiles, and provides their embarked units with +5 Strength Combat Strength and +2 Movement. Unimproved Woods and Rainforests in their territory provide extra Production (+1 initially, with additional bonuses after discovering Mercantilism and Conservation), and Fishing Boats provide +1 Food and trigger a Culture Bomb; however, they cannot harvest resources or earn Great Writer Great Writers. Their unique unit is the Toa (which replaces the Swordsman), and their unique building is the Marae (which replaces the Amphitheater).
