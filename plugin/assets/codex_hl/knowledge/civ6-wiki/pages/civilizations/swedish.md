## Summary

Sweden
The Swedish people (or Swedes) represent a civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. They are led by Kristina, under whom their default colors are light blue and yellow.
The Swedes' civilization ability is Nobel Prize, which grants them additional Scientist and Engineer points from Universities and Factories, generates 50 Diplomatic Favor for each Great Person they recruit and adds three unique World Congress competitions from the Industrial Era onwards. Their unique unit is the Carolean (which replaces the Pike and Shot), and their unique tile improvement is the Open-Air Museum.
