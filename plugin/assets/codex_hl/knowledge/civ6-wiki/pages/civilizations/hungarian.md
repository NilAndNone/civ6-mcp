## Summary

Hungary
The Hungarian people represent a civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. They are led by Matthias Corvinus, under whom their default colors are dark green and orange.
The Hungarians' civilization ability is Pearl of the Danube, which provides a 50% Production bonus when constructing District and buildings across a River from a City Center. Their unique unit is the Huszár (which replaces the Cavalry), and their unique building is the Thermal Bath (which replaces the Zoo).
