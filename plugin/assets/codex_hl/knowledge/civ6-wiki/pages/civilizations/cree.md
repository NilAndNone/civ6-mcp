## Summary

Cree
The Cree people represent a civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. They are led by Poundmaker, under whom their default colors are dark blue and green.
The Cree's civilization ability is Nîhithaw, which provides them with +1 Trade Route capacity and a free Trader after researching Pottery and gives them control of any unclaimed tiles within 3 tiles of their cities when a Trader passes through them. Their unique unit is the Okihtcitaw (which replaces the Scout), and their unique tile improvement is the Mekewap.
