## Summary

Zoroastrianism
Zoroastrianism is one of the twelve default religions in Civilization VI. Players who have founded a pantheon can found Zoroastrianism by using a Great Prophet at either a Holy Site or Stonehenge.
Zoroastrian places of worship are represented by the Dar-e Mehr in the game, but there is no in-game relationship between the religion and the building.
