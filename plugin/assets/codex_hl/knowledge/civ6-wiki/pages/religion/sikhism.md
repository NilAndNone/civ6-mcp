## Summary

Sikhism
Sikhism is one of the twelve default religions in Civilization VI. Players who have founded a pantheon can found Sikhism by using a Great Prophet at either a Holy Site or Stonehenge.
Sikh places of worship are represented by the Gurdwara in the game, but there is no in-game relationship between the religion and the building.
