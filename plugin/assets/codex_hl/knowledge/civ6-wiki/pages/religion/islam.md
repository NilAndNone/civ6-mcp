## Summary

Islam
Islam is one of the twelve default religions in Civilization VI. Players who have founded a pantheon can found Islam by using a Great Prophet at either a Holy Site or Stonehenge.
Islamic places of worship are represented by the Mosque in the game, but there is no in-game relationship between the religion and the building.

## Key Facts

- followers: Mansa Musa, Nader Shah, Saladin, Suleiman, Sundiata Keita
