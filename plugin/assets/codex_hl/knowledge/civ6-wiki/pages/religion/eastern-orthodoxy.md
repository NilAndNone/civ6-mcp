## Summary

Eastern Orthodox Church
Eastern Orthodoxy is one of the twelve default religions in Civilization VI. Players who have founded a pantheon can found Eastern Orthodoxy by using a Great Prophet at either a Holy Site or Stonehenge.
Eastern Orthodox places of worship are represented by the Cathedral in the game, but there is no in-game relationship between the religion and the building.

## Key Facts

- followers: Basil II,Harald Hardrada (Varangian),Menelik II,Peter,Tamar,Theodora
