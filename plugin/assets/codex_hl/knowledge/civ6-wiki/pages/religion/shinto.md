## Summary

Shinto
Shinto is one of the twelve default religions in Civilization VI. Players who have founded a pantheon can found Shinto by using a Great Prophet at either a Holy Site or Stonehenge.
Shinto places of worship are represented by the Pagoda in the game, but there is no in-game relationship between the religion and the building.
