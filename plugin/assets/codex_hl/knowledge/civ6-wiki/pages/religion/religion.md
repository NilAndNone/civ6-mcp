Religion is a returning feature in Civilization VI, but its role has been greatly expanded in both scope and importance, becoming the focus of a new victory condition. A religion is a system of spiritual beliefs (manifested by game bonuses), founded by a Great Prophet, and spread among the population of cities around the world. Religion is primarily based around Faith, which you can gain through various means including buildings, wonders, and others.
