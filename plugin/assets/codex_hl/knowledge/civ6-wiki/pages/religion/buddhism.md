## Summary

Buddhism
Buddhism is one of the twelve default religions in Civilization VI. Players who have founded a pantheon can found Buddhism by using a Great Prophet at either a Holy Site or Stonehenge.
Buddhist places of worship are represented by the Pagoda, the Wat, and the Stupa in the game, but there is no in-game relationship between the religion and the buildings.

## Key Facts
