## Summary

The Persona Packs were DLC for Civilization VI, released at the same time as the Ethiopia Pack, the July 2020 component of Civilization VI: New Frontier Pass. The Persona Packs are not included in the Ethiopia Pack, but freely available to all players who bought the New Frontier Pass. They included alternate personas for two leaders: Teddy Roosevelt and Catherine de Medici.
