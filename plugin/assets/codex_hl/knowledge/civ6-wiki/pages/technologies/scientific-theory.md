## Summary

Scientific Theory is an Industrial Era technology in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by developing The Enlightenment civic.

## Key Facts

- Name: Scientific Theory
- Infrastructure: Oxford University
- notes: Allows Research Agreement diplomatic action; +1 Food from the Plantation improvement.
- Cost: 845 Science Icon; 930
- Requires: Astronomy, Banking
- Leads to: Sanitation, Economics, Flight
- Eureka: Have The Enlightenment civic.
- quote: Claims that cannot be tested, assertions immune to disproof are veridically worthless, whatever value they may have in inspiring us or exciting our sense of wonder.
- quoted: Carl Sagan
