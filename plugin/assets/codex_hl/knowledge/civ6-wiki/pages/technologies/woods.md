## Summary

Woods (sometimes called Forests) are a terrain feature in Civilization VI. They commonly appear on flat and hilly Grassland, Plains, and Tundra tiles.
* Yields:
** +1 Production.
* Movement needed:
** 2 Moves Icon MPs (3 if on Hills)
* Defense provided:
** +3 (+6 if on Hills)
* Additional traits:
** Can receive a special tile improvement, the Lumber Mill.
** +1 Appeal
** +0.5 Faith adjacency bonus to Holy Site Districts (rounded down).
