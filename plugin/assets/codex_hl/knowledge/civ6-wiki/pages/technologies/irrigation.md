## Summary

Irrigation is an Ancient Era technology in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by building a Farm on any bonus resource.

## Key Facts

- Name: Irrigation
- Era: Ancient
- Cost: 50
- Eureka: Build a Farm on a Bonus resource.
- Requires: Pottery
- Infrastructure: Hanging Gardens, Plantation, Stepwell, Palgum
- notes: Allows clearing of Marsh and harvesting of Bananas.
- quote: Thousands have lived without love, not one without water.
