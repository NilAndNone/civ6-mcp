## Summary

A Marsh is a terrain feature in Civilization VI. It is found on flat Grassland tiles.
* Yields:
** +1 Food.
* Movement needed:
** 2 Moves Icon MPs
* Defense provided:
** -2 penalty
* Additional traits:
** -1 Appeal
** Cannot be improved, unless there is a resource on it
** +1 Science with a Zoo
** +2 Science & +1 Production with Etemenanki
** +1 Production with Lady of the Reeds and Marshes pantheon ( +2 Production)
