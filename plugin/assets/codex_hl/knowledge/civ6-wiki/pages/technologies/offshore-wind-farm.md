## Summary

The Offshore Wind Farm is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It is used to supply Power to the city from water tiles. It must be built on a Coast or Lake tile.
* Effects:
** +2 Production.
** +1 Gold.
** +2 Power
