# Oil Well

- Category: technologies

## Summary

The Oil Well is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI. It is used to access land-based Oil resources.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Oil Well is unlocked with Refining.
* Effects:
** +2 Production
** +1 Production and +1 Faith with God of Craftsmen pantheon
** +1 Production (requires Predictive Systems)
** -1 Appeal
