## Summary

Apprenticeship is a Medieval Era technology in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by building any three Mines.

## Key Facts

- Era: Medieval
- Name: Apprenticeship
- Cost: 300
- Requires: Currency, Horseback Riding
- Leads to: Education, Gunpowder
- Eureka: Build 3 Mines.
- Units: Man-At-Arms
- Infrastructure: Industrial Zone, Hansa, Workshop
- notes: +1 Production to the Mine improvement.
