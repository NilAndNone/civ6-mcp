# Colossal Heads

- Category: technologies
- Source: [https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Colossal_Heads_%28Civ6%29](https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Colossal_Heads_%28Civ6%29)
- Aliases: Colossal Heads

## Summary

Olmec colossal heads
Colossal Heads are a special tile improvement in Civilization VI. They can be built by a player that is the Suzerain of the La Venta city-state.

## Key Facts

- unique: La Venta
- Unique unit: Builder
- req: Suzerain
- Plunder yield: Faith
- effect: +2 Faith
+1 Culture Humanism (Vanilla and only)
+1 Faith for every 2 adjacent Woods or Rainforest.
Provides Tourism equal to its Culture output Flight (Vanilla and only)
 Provides Tourism equal to its Faith output Flight
- terrain: Volcanic Soil, Desert, Desert (Hills), Plains, Plains (Hills), Grassland, Grassland (Hills), Tundra, Tundra (Hills)
