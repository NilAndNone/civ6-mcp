## Summary

Kampung
The Kampung is a unique tile improvement of the Indonesian civilization in Civilization VI. It must be built on Coast or Lake tiles adjacent to sea resources (except Shipwrecks).
* Effects:
** +1 Production
** +1 Housing
** +1 Food from every adjacent Fishing Boats
** Provides Tourism from Food after researching Flight
** +1 Housing (with Mass Production)
** +1 Production (with Civil Engineering)
