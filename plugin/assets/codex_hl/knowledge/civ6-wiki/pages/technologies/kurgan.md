## Summary

Kurgan
The Kurgan is a unique tile improvement of the Scythian civilization in Civilization VI.
* Effects:
** +1 Faith
** +3 Gold
** +1 Faith for each adjacent Pasture, increasing to +2 Faith for each adjacent Pasture (with Stirrups)
** Provides Tourism equal to its Faith output with Flight.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built on Hills tiles.
