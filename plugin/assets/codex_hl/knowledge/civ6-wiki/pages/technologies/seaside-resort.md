## Summary

The Seaside Resort is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI. It can only be built on flat coastal Grassland, Plains, and Desert tiles with a minimum Appeal rating of Breathtaking (4+). In Gathering Storm, it can also be built on any coastal Volcanic Soil tile that has Breathtaking Appeal, regardless of its terrain or relief.
* Effects:
** Provides Tourism equal to the Appeal of the tile on which it was built.
** Provides Gold equal to the Appeal of the tile on which it was built.
