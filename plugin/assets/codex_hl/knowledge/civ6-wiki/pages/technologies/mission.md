## Summary

Mission (station)
The Mission is a unique tile improvement of the Spanish civilization in Civilization VI.
* Effects:
** +2 Faith
** +2 additional Faith, +1 Food, and +1 Production if on a different continent than your Capital
** +1 Science for every adjacent Campus or Holy Site
** +2 Science (requires Cultural Heritage)
** +2 Loyalty per turn for cities with a Mission next to the City Center and not on the original Capital's continent
