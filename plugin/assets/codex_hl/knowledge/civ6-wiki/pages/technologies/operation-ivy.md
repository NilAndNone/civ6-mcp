## Summary

Operation Ivy
Operation Ivy is a project in Civilization VI. It becomes available after researching Nuclear Fusion and completing the Manhattan Project.
Operation Ivy applies the theoretical knowledge of Nuclear Fusion to the practical task of building even deadlier doomsday weapons. Once completed, it allows the player to undertake the Build Thermonuclear Device project.
