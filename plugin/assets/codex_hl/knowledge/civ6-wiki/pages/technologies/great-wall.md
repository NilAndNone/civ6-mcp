## Summary

Great Wall of China
The Great Wall is a unique tile improvement of the Chinese civilization in Civilization VI. It can only be built in a line on the current border of your territory.

## Key Facts

- unique: Chinese
- Plunder yield: Gold
- req: Masonry
- effect: Occupying unit receives +4 Strength Defense Strength and 2 turns of fortification;
+2 Gold from each adjacent Great Wall;
+2 Culture from each adjacent Great Wall Castles;
Provides Tourism equal to its Culture output Flight;
 Can only be pillaged but never removed by disasters
- terrain: Volcanic Soil, Grassland, Grassland (Hills), Plains, Plains (Hills), Desert, Desert (Hills), Tundra, Tundra (Hills), Snow, Snow (Hills)
