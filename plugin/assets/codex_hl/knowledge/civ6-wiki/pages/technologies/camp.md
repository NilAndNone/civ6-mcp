## Summary

The Camp is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI. It is used to improve certain wildlife resources.
* Effects:
** +1 Gold ( +2 Gold)
** +0.5 Housing
** +1 Food and +1 Production (requires Mercantilism)
** +1 additional Gold (requires Synthetic Materials) ( +2 Gold)
** +1 Food and +1 Production with Goddess of the Hunt pantheon
** Wilfrid Laurier only: +2 Food when built on Tundra and Tundra Hills
* Access:
** Bonus Resources: Deer
** Luxury Resources: Furs, Ivory, Truffles, Honey
