## Summary

The Solar Farm is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It is used to supply renewable Power to its city from flat terrain tiles. It cannot be built on Snow.
* Effects:
** +1 Production.
** +1 Gold.
** +2 Power.
