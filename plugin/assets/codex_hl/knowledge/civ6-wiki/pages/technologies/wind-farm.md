## Summary

The Wind Farm is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It is used to supply Power to its city from a renewable source: the wind. It can only be constructed on Hills tiles.
* Effects:
** +1 Production.
** +2 Gold.
** +2 Power.
