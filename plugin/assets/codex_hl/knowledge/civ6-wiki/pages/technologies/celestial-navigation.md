## Summary

Celestial Navigation (civic) (Civ6)
Celestial Navigation is a Classical Era technology in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by building Fishing Boats on any 2 sea resources.

## Key Facts

- Era: Classical
- Name: Celestial Navigation
- Infrastructure: Great Lighthouse, Lighthouse, Harbor, Royal Navy Dockyard, Cothon
- notes: Allows Traders to embark, and allows harvesting of Fish and Crabs.
- Cost: 120
- Requires: Sailing, Astrology
- Eureka: Improve 2 Sea Resources.
