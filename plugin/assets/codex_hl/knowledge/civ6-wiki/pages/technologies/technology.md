Technologies represent new scientific advancements or inventions for your civilization. Each technology you research unlocks new buildings, units, or other advancements, and expands the power of your empire.
Technologies are developed through a continuous research process. the key to which is the Science yield. However, they can also be "boosted" through Eurekas. For more information, see the Science article.
