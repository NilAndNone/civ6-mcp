## Summary

The Offshore Oil Rig is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI. It is used to access sea-based Oil resources.
* Effects:
** +2 Production
** +1 Production (requires Predictive Systems)
** +1 Production and +1 Faith with God of Craftsmen pantheon
** -1 Appeal
