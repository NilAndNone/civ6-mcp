## Summary

Interstellar travel
Exoplanet Expedition is a project in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm and the fourth step toward winning a Science Victory. It becomes available after researching Smart Materials and completing the Launch Mars Colony project, and requires a Spaceport district.
The exoplanet expedition brings the space pioneer beyond the borders of our solar system! Once completed, it triggers the fifth and final Science Victory step: it launches a spacecraft that travels to a distant star system (50 Light Years away on Standard speed) at a base speed of 1 Light Year per turn. When the spacecraft arrives at its destination, the player wins.
