## Summary

Convert to Nuclear Power is a project in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It becomes available after building an Industrial Zone district (or one of its replacements) and researching Nuclear Fission.
A city must have a Coal Power Plant or an Oil Power Plant to undertake this project. When completed, it removes the existing Power Plant and replaces it with a Nuclear Power Plant.
