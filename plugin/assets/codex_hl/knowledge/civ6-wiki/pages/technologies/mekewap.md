## Summary

Wigwam
The Mekewap is a unique tile improvement of the Cree civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It must be built adjacent to a Bonus or Luxury Resource.
* Effects:
** +1 Production
** +1 Housing
** +1 Gold if adjacent to a luxury resource
** +1 additional Housing (with Civil Service).
** +1 additional Production (with Civil Service).
** +2 additional Gold for each adjacent Luxury Resource (with Cartography).
** +1 Food for every two adjacent Bonus Resources, increasing to +1 Food for each adjacent Bonus Resource (with Conservation).
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be adjacent to another Mekewap.
