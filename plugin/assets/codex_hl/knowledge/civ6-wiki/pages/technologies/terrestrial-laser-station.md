## Summary

Laser propulsion
Terrestrial Laser Station is a project in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It becomes available after researching Offworld Mission and completing the Exoplanet Expedition project, and requires a Spaceport district.
This project builds a high-tech laser station that shoots an ultracharged high-precision beam toward the exoplanet spacecraft to power it up. It increases the speed of the Exoplanet Expedition by 1 Light Year per turn, accelerating the player's Science Victory progress.
This project may be completed multiple times. However, it increases the city's Power requirement by 5 each time it is completed, and will cease to provide its bonus if the city is not powered.
