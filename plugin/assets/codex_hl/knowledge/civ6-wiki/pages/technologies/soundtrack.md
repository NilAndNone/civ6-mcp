## Summary

video]]
The soundtrack of Civilization VI was written and orchestrated primarily by Geoff Knorr, who was assisted by Roland Rizzo, Griffin Cohen, and Phill Boucher. The themes of each civilization are played as different variations of the same theme throughout the eras, with simple single instrument melodies in the Ancient Era turning into modern compositions by the Atomic Era, symbolizing the evolution and growth of one's civilization throughout the ages.
