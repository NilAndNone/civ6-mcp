## Summary

Sputnik 1
Launch Earth Satellite is a project in Civilization VI and the first step toward winning a Science Victory. It becomes available after researching Rocketry and requires a Spaceport district.
Through this project man makes the first bold step into space. Once completed, it launches a satellite into space that confirms the player's ability to conduct space travel and reveals the entire map.
