## Summary

The Plantation is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI. It may only be built on non-grain plant resources.
* Effects:
** +2 Gold
** +0.5 Housing
** +1 Food (requires Feudalism)
** +1 Food (requires Scientific Theory)
** +2 Gold (requires Globalization)
** +1 Culture with Goddess of Festivals pantheon
** +1 Production for every 2 adjacent Haciendas (increasing to +1 Production for every Hacienda with Rapid Deployment)
* Access:
** Bonus Resources: Bananas
** Luxury Resources: Citrus, Cocoa, Cotton, Coffee, Dyes, Incense, Olives , Silk, Spices, Sugar, Tea, Tobacco, Wine
