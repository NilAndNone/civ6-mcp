## Summary

The Airstrip is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI. It provides a base for military aircraft and may be built on flat terrain in your own or neutral territory.
* Effects:
** +3 aircraft slots
** -1 Appeal

## Key Facts

- req: Flight
- Plunder yield: None

- Unique unit: Military Engineer
