## Summary

Rock-Hewn Churches, LalibelaMonolithic church
The Rock-Hewn Church is a unique tile improvement of the Ethiopian civilization in Civilization VI. It must be built on Hills or Volcanic Soil tiles.
* Effects:
** +1 Faith
** +1 Faith for every adjacent Mountain and Hills tile
** Provides Tourism from Faith after researching Flight
** +1 Appeal to adjacent tiles
** Can never be destroyed (only pillaged) by natural disasters
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be adjacent to another Rock-Hewn Church.
