## Summary

Particle Beam Siege Cannon is a "Promotion" for the Giant Death Robot in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. Since GDRs cannot gain XP or levels, it is automatically conferred upon every GDR a player owns or builds after researching Advanced Power Cells.
