## Summary

Goddess of the Hunt is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It increases the Food output of all Camps by 1. In Gathering Storm, it also increases the Production output of all Camps by 1.

## Key Facts

- Name: Goddess of the Hunt
- effect: +1 Food from Camps.
 +1 Food and +1 Production from Camps.
