## Summary

Initiation Rites is a pantheon in Civilization VI. Whenever a player with this pantheon clears a Barbarian Outpost, that player receives +50 Faith. In Gathering Storm, it was altered to fully heal the unit that clears the outpost as well.

## Key Facts

- Name: Initiation Rites
- effect: +50 Faith for each Barbarian Outpost cleared.
 +50 Faith for each Barbarian Outpost cleared. The unit that cleared the Barbarian Outpost heals +100 HP.
