## Summary

Dance of the Aurora is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It increases the Faith output of Holy Site districts by 1 for each adjacent Tundra tile.

## Key Facts

- Name: Dance of the Aurora
- effect: Holy Site districts get +1 Faith from adjacent Tundra tiles.
