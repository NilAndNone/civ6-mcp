## Summary

God of Healing is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It increases units' healing by 30 in Holy Site districts and their adjacent tiles, even for those that have moved.

## Key Facts

- Name: God of Healing
- effect: Increases units' healing by 30 in Holy Site districts, or any adjacent tiles.
