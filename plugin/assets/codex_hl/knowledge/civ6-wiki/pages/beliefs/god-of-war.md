## Summary

God of War (Apedemak) (Civ6)
God of War is a pantheon in Civilization VI. Whenever a player with this pantheon kills a military unit within 8 tiles of a Holy Site district, they are awarded with bonus Faith equal to 50% of the Strength Combat Strength of the defeated unit. (For instance, killing an AT Crew, which has a Strength Strength of 70, provides 35 Faith.)
Note that this pantheon's bonus does not apply to kills from theological combat, since religious units have no Strength Combat Strength.

## Key Facts

- Name: God of War
- effect: Bonus Faith equal to 50% of the strength of each combat unit killed within 8 tiles of a Holy Site district.
