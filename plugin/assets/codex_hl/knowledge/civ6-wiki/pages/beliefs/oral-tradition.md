## Summary

Oral Tradition was a pantheon in Civilization VI. It granted +1 Culture to all Plantation improvements built on tiles with Bananas, Citrus, Cotton, Dyes, Silk, Spices, or Sugar. This pantheon was removed from Gathering Storm in the June 2019 Update.

## Key Facts
