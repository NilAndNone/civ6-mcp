## Summary

Goddess of the Harvest was a pantheon in Civilization VI. When a player with the Goddess of the Harvest pantheon harvested a resource or removed a feature, they received Faith equal to the other yield's quantity. This pantheon was removed from Gathering Storm in the June 2019 Update.

## Key Facts

- Name: Goddess of the Harvest
- removed: Gathering Storm
- effect: Harvesting a resource or removing a feature receives Faith equal to the other yield's quantity.
