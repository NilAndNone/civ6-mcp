## Summary

Monument to the Gods is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It provides a 15% Production bonus when building wonders from the Ancient and Classical Eras.

## Key Facts

- Name: Monument to the Gods
- effect: +15% Production towards Ancient and Classical era Wonders.
