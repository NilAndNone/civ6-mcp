## Summary

Earth Goddess is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It adds 1 Faith to all tiles owned by the player that have Charming or Breathtaking Appeal. In Gathering Storm, it was altered to add 1 Faith to all tiles owned by the player that have Breathtaking Appeal only.

## Key Facts

- Name: Earth Goddess
- effect: +1 Faith from tiles with Charming or Breathtaking Appeal.
 +1 Faith from tiles with Breathtaking Appeal.
