## Summary

Fertility Rites is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It increases the growth rate of all cities by 10%. In Gathering Storm, it also provides a free Builder in the founder's Capital.

## Key Facts

- Name: Fertility Rites
- effect: City growth rate is 10% higher.
 When chosen receive a Builder in your capital. City growth rate is 10% higher.
