## Summary

Lady of the Reeds and Marshes is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It increases the Production of Marsh, Oasis, and Desert Floodplains tiles by 1 (2 in Gathering Storm).

## Key Facts

- Name: Lady of the Reeds and Marshes
