## Summary

God of Craftsmen is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It provides +1 Production to all Mine improvements built on strategic resources (i.e., Iron, Niter, Coal, Aluminum, and Uranium). In Gathering Storm, it provides +1 Production and +1 Faith for all improved strategic resources.

## Key Facts

- Name: God of Craftsmen
- effect: +1 Production from Mines over Strategic resources.
 +1 Production and +1 Faith from improved Strategic resources.
