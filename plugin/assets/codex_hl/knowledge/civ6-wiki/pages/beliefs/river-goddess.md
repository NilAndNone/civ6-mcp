## Summary

River Goddess is a pantheon in Civilization VI. It provides +1 Amenity to cities if they have a Holy Site district adjacent to a River. In Gathering Storm, it was altered to provide +2 Amenities and +2 Housing to cities instead.

## Key Facts

- Name: River Goddess
- effect: +1 Amenity to cities if they have a Holy Site district adjacent to a River.
 +2 Amenities and +2 Housing to cities if they have a Holy Site district adjacent to a River.
