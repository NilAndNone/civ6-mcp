## Summary

Theocracy is a form of Tier 2 government in Civilization VI. It is unlocked by the Reformed Church civic.
In Rise and Fall, this government's legacy bonus is conferred by Theocratic Legacy, a Wildcard policy unlocked by changing governments after adopting Theocracy and constructing a Tier 2 government building (Foreign Ministry, Grand Master's Chapel, Intelligence Agency, or Queen's Bibliotheque).
