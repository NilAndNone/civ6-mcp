## Summary

Moai
The Moai is a special tile improvement in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It can be built by a player that is the Suzerain of the Rapa Nui city-state.
* Effects:
** +1 Culture.
** +1 Culture for every 2 adjacent Moai (increasing to +1 Culture for every adjacent Moai with Medieval Faires).
** +2 Culture if on or adjacent to a Volcanic Soil tile.
** +1 Culture if adjacent to a Coast or Lake tile.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be placed adjacent to a Woods or Rainforest tile.
