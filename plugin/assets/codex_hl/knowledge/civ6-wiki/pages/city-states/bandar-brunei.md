## Summary

Bandar Seri Begawan
Bandar Brunei is a trade city-state in Civilization VI. It replaced Jakarta under all rulesets in the Khmer and Indonesia Civilization & Scenario Pack.

## Key Facts

- type: Trade
- color: Yellow
- 1envoy: +4 Gold in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +4 Gold in every Commercial Hub district.
 +4 Gold in every Market building.
- 6envoys: +8 Gold in every Commercial Hub district.
 +4 Gold in every Bank building.
- unique bonus: Your Trading Posts in foreign cities provide +1 Gold to your Trade Routes passing through or going to the city.
