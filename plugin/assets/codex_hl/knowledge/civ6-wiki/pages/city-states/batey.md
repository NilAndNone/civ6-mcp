## Summary

Batey (game)
The Batey is a special tile improvement in Civilization VI. It can be built by a player that is the Suzerain of the Caguana city-state.
* Effects:
** +1 Culture.
** +1 Culture for every adjacent Bonus Resource or Entertainment Complex (increasing to +2 Culture with Exploration).
** Provides Tourism after researching Flight.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built on Hills tiles or adjacent to another Batey.
