## Summary

Vatican City
Vatican City is a religious city-state in Civilization VI. It was added in the Maya & Gran Colombia Pack.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Maya & Gran Colombia Pack
- type: Religious
- color: White
- 1envoy: +2 Faith in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +2 Faith in every Holy Site district.
 +2 Faith in every Shrine building.
- 6envoys: +4 Faith in every Holy Site district.
 +2 Faith in every Temple building.
- unique bonus: When the player activates a Great Person, they spread 400 religious pressure of the player’s founded (or majority) religion to cities within 10 tiles.
