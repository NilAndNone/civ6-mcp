## Summary

Stockholm
Stockholm is a scientific city-state in Civilization VI.
Under rulesets in which Sweden is a playable civilization, Stockholm becomes a Swedish city and is replaced with Bologna.

## Key Facts

- removed: Gathering Storm
- type: Scientific
- color: Blue
- 1envoy: +2 Science in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +2 Science in every Campus district.
 +2 Science in every Library building.
- 6envoys: +4 Science in every Campus district.
 +2 Science in every University building.
- unique bonus: Your districts provide +1 Great Person point of their type (Writer, Artist, and Musician for Theater Square districts).
 Your districts with a building provide +1 Great Person point of their type (Writer, Artist, and Musician for Theater Square districts with a building).
