## Summary

Seoul
Seoul is a scientific city-state in Civilization VI.
Under rulesets in which Korea is a playable civilization, Seoul becomes a Korean city and is replaced with Anshan.

## Key Facts

- removed: Rise and Fall
- type: Scientific
- color: Blue
- 1envoy: +2 Science in the Capital
- 3envoys: +2 Science in every Campus district
- 6envoys: +4 Science in every Campus district
- unique bonus: When you enter a new era, earn a random Eureka from that era.
