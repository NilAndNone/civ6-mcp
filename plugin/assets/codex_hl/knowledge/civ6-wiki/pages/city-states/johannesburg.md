## Summary

Johannesburg
Johannesburg is an industrial city-state in Civilization VI. It was added in the Babylon Pack.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Babylon Pack
- type: Industrial
- color: Brown
- 1envoy: +2 Production in the Capital when producing wonders, buildings, and districts
- 3envoys: +2 Production in every Industrial Zone district when producing wonders, buildings, and districts.
 +2 Production in every city with a Workshop building when producing wonders, buildings, and districts.
- 6envoys: +4 Production in every Industrial Zone district when producing wonders, buildings, and districts.
 +2 Production in every city with a Factory building when producing wonders, buildings, and districts.
- unique bonus: Cities receive +1 Production for every improved resource type. After researching Industrialization it becomes +2 Production.
