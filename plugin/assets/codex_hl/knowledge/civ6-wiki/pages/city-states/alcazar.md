## Summary

Alcázar
The Alcázar is a special tile improvement in Civilization VI. It can be built by a player that is the Suzerain of the Granada city-state.
* Effects:
** +2 Culture
** Occupying unit receives +4 Strength Defense Strength and automatically gains 2 turns of fortification (+6 Strength Defense Strength).
** Provides Science equal to 50% of the tile's Appeal.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be adjacent to another Alcázar.
In the Zombie Defense game mode, the Alcázar deals moderate (15 HP) damage to hostile units passing through it.
