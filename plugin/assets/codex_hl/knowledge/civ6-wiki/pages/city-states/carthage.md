## Summary

Carthage
Carthage is a militaristic city-state in Civilization VI.
Under rulesets in which Phoenicia is a playable civilization, Carthage becomes a Phoenician city and is replaced with Ngazargamu.

## Key Facts

- removed: Gathering Storm
- type: Militaristic
- color: Red
- 1envoy: +2 Production in the Capital when producing units.
- 3envoys: +2 Production in every Encampment district when producing units.
 +2 Production in every Barracks or Stable building when producing units.
- 6envoys: +4 Production in every Encampment district when producing units.
 +2 Production in every Armory building when producing units.
- unique bonus: Each Encampment in your cities provides an extra Trade Route.
 Land combat units are 20% cheaper to purchase with Gold for each Encampment district building in that city.
