## Summary

Amsterdam
Amsterdam is a trade city-state in Civilization VI.
Under rulesets in which the Netherlands is a playable civilization, Amsterdam becomes a Dutch city and is replaced with Venice.

## Key Facts

- removed: Rise and Fall
- type: Trade
- color: Yellow
- 1envoy: +4 Gold in the Capital
- 3envoys: +4 Gold in every Commercial Hub district
- 6envoys: Additional +4 Gold in every Commercial Hub district
- unique bonus: Your Trade Routes to foreign cities earn +1 Gold for each luxury resource.
