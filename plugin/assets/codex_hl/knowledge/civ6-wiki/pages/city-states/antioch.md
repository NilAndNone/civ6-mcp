## Summary

Antioch
Antioch is a trade city-state in Civilization VI. It replaced Amsterdam in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall.
In the Byzantium & Gaul Pack, with the introduction of Byzantium as a playable civilization, Antioch became a Byzantine city and was replaced with Venice under all rulesets.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Rise and Fall
- removed: all
- type: Trade
- color: Yellow
- 1envoy: +4 Gold in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +4 Gold in every Market building.
- 6envoys: +4 Gold in every Bank building.
- unique bonus: Your Trade Routes to foreign cities earn +1 Gold for each Luxury resource at the destination.
