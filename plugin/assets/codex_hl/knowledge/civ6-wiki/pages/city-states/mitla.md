## Summary

Mitla is a scientific city-state in Civilization VI. It replaced Palenque under all rulesets in the Maya & Gran Colombia Pack.

- type: Scientific

- 3envoys: +2 Science in every Campus district.

- 6envoys: +4 Science in every Campus district.
