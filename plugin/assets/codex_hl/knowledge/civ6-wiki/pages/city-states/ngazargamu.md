## Summary

Ngazargamu
Ngazargamu is a militaristic city-state in Civilization VI. It replaces Carthage under rulesets in which Phoenicia is a playable civilization.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Gathering Storm
- type: Militaristic
- color: Red
- 1envoy: +2 Production in the Capital when producing units.
- 3envoys: +2 Production in every Barracks or Stable building when producing units.
- 6envoys: +2 Production in every Armory building when producing units.
- unique bonus: Land combat or Support units are 20% cheaper to purchase with Gold for each Encampment district building in that city.
