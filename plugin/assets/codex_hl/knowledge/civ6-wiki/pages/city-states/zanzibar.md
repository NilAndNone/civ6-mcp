## Summary

Zanzibar City
Zanzibar is a trade city-state in Civilization VI.

## Key Facts

- type: Trade
- color: Yellow
- 1envoy: +4 Gold in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +4 Gold in every Commercial Hub district
 +4 Gold in every Market building.
- 6envoys: +8 Gold in every Commercial Hub district
 +4 Gold in every Bank building.
- unique bonus: Receive the Cinnamon and Cloves Luxury resources. These cannot be earned any other way in the game, and provide 6 Amenities each.
