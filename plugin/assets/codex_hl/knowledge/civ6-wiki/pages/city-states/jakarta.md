## Summary

Jakarta
Jakarta is a trade city-state in Civilization VI.
In the Khmer and Indonesia Civilization & Scenario Pack, with the introduction of Indonesia as a playable civilization, Jakarta became an Indonesian city and was replaced with Bandar Brunei under all rulesets.

## Key Facts

- removed: all
- type: Trade
- color: Yellow
- 1envoy: +4 Gold in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +4 Gold in every Commercial Hub district.
- 6envoys: +8 Gold in every Commercial Hub district.
- unique bonus: Your Trading Posts at international destinations grant +1 additional Gold to all Trade Routes that pass through them.
