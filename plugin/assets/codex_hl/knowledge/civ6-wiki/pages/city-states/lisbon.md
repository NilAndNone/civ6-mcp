## Summary

Lisbon
Lisbon is a trade city-state in Civilization VI.
In the Portugal Pack, with the introduction of Portugal as a playable civilization, Lisbon became a Portuguese city and was replaced with Mogadishu under all rulesets.

## Key Facts

- removed: all
- type: Trade
- color: Yellow
- 1envoy: +4 Gold in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +4 Gold in every Commercial Hub district.
 +4 Gold in every Market building.
- 6envoys: +8 Gold in every Commercial Hub district.
 +4 Gold in every Bank building.
- unique bonus: Your Trader units are immune to being plundered on water tiles.
