## Summary

Mexico City
Mexico City is an industrial city-state in Civilization VI. It replaces Toronto under rulesets in which Canada is a playable civilization.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Gathering Storm
- type: Industrial
- color: Brown
- 1envoy: +2 Production in the Capital when producing wonders, buildings, and districts.
- 3envoys: +2 Production in every city with a Workshop building when producing wonders, buildings, and districts.
- 6envoys: +2 Production in every city with a Factory building when producing wonders, buildings, and districts.
- unique bonus: Regional effects from your Industrial Zone, Entertainment Complex and Water Park districts reach 3 tiles farther.
