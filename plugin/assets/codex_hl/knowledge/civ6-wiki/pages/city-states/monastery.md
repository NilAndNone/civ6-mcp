## Summary

Monastery
The Monastery is a special tile improvement in Civilization VI. It can be built by a player that is the Suzerain of the Armagh city-state.
* Effects:
** +2 Faith
** Provides +15 HP healing every turn for friendly religious units.
** +1 Faith for every 2 adjacent Districts.
** +1 Housing.
** +1 additional Housing (with Colonialism).
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be adjacent to another Monastery.
