## Summary

Wolin (town)
Wolin is a militaristic city-state in Civilization VI. It was added in the Babylon Pack.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Babylon Pack
- type: Militaristic
- color: Red
- 1envoy: +2 Production in the Capital when producing units.
- 3envoys: +2 Production in every Encampment district when producing units.
 +2 Production in every Barracks or Stable building when producing units.
- 6envoys: +4 Production in every Encampment district when producing units.
 +2 Production in every Armory building when producing units.
- unique bonus: Receive General points when a land unit defeats a major or minor civilization's unit and receive Admiral points when a naval unit defeats a major or minor civilization's unit equal to 25% of the opposing unit's strength (Standard Speed).
