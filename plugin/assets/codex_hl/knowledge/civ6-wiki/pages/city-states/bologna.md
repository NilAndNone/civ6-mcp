## Summary

Bologna
Bologna is a scientific city-state in Civilization VI. It replaces Stockholm under rulesets in which Sweden is a playable civilization.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Gathering Storm
- type: Scientific
- color: Blue
- 1envoy: +2 Science in the Capital. ; Ethiopia Pack: +1 Science in the Capital and in every Library building.
- 3envoys: +2 Science in every Library building. ; Ethiopia Pack: +2 Science in every University and Consulate building.
- 6envoys: +2 Science in every University building. ; Ethiopia Pack: +3 Science in every Research Lab and Chancery building.
- unique bonus: Your districts with a building provide +1 Great Person point of their type (Writer, Artist, and Musician for Theater Square districts with a building).
