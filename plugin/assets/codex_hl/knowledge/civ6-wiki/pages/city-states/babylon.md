## Summary

Babylon
Babylonian (Civ6)
Babylon is a scientific city-state in Civilization VI. It replaced Seoul in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall.
The fourth DLC pack of New Frontier Pass introduces the Babylonian civilization. Because Babylon is the capital city of that civilization, the city-state this article refers to was replaced with Anshan under all rulesets.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Rise and Fall
- removed: all
- type: Scientific
- color: Blue
- 1envoy: +2 Science in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +2 Science in every Library building.
- 6envoys: +2 Science in every University building.
- unique bonus: +2 Science from each Writing Great Work of Writing. +1 Science from each Relic Relic and Artifact Artifact.
