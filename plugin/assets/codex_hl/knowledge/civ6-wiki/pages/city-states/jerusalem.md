## Summary

Jerusalem
Jerusalem is a religious city-state in Civilization VI.

## Key Facts

- type: Religious
- color: White
- 1envoy: +2 Faith in the Capital.
- 3envoys: +2 Faith in every Holy Site district.
 +2 Faith in every Shrine building.
- 6envoys: +4 Faith in every Holy Site district.
 +2 Faith in every Temple building.
- unique bonus: Automatically converts to the Religion you founded and exerts pressure for that religion as if it were a Holy City.
 Your cities with Holy Sites exert pressure as if they were Holy Cities (4x religious pressure on all cities within 10 tiles).
