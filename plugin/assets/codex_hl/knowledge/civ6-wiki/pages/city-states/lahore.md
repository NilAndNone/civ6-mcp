## Summary

Lahore
Lahore is a militaristic city-state in Civilization VI. It was added in the Maya & Gran Colombia Pack.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Maya & Gran Colombia Pack
- type: Militaristic
- color: Red
- 1envoy: +2 Production in the Capital when producing units.
- 3envoys: +2 Production in every Barracks or Stable building when producing units.
- 6envoys: +2 Production in every Armory building when producing units.
- unique bonus: Suzerains of this City-State can purchase the new unique Nihang unit with Faith.
