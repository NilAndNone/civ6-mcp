## Summary

Anshan (Persia)
Anshan is a scientific city-state in Civilization VI. It replaced Babylon in the Babylon Pack and replaces Seoul under rulesets in which Korea is a playable civilization.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Rise and Fall
- type: Scientific
- color: Blue
- 1envoy: +2 Science in the Capital. ; Ethiopia Pack: +1 Science in the Capital and in every Library building.
- 3envoys: +2 Science in every Campus district.

- 6envoys: +4 Science in every Campus district.
 +2 Science in every University building. ; Ethiopia Pack: +3 Science in every Research Lab and Chancery building.
