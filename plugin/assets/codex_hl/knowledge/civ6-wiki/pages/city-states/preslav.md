## Key Facts

- type: Militaristic
- color: Red
- 1envoy: +2 Production in the Capital when producing units.
- 3envoys: +2 Production in every Encampment district when producing units.
 +2 Production in every city with a Barracks or Stable building when producing units.
- 6envoys: +4 Production in every Encampment district when producing units.
 +2 Production in every city with an Armory building when producing units.
- unique bonus: Your light and heavy cavalry units have +5 Strength Strength when fighting on Hills tiles.
 You receive +2 Loyalty per turn in cities for each Encampment district building.
