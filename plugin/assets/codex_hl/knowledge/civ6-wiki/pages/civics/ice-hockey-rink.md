## Summary

Ice hockey rink
The Ice Hockey Rink is a unique tile improvement of the Canadian civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It must be built on Tundra, Tundra Hills, Snow, or Snow Hills tiles.
* Effects:
** +1 Amenity
** +2 Appeal to adjacent tiles
** +1 Culture for each adjacent Tundra, Tundra Hills, Snow, and Snow Hills tile
** +2 Food and +2 Production (with Professional Sports)
** +4 Culture if adjacent to a Stadium
* Restrictions:
** Limit of one per city.
** Tiles with Ice Hockey Rinks cannot be swapped.
