## Summary

Mobilization is a Modern Era civic in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by owning 3 Corps in your military.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- Name: Mobilization
- notes: Allows formation of Defensive Pacts. Allows forming three identical units into an Army or Armada; one new Casus Belli that can be used to justify wars: War of Territorial Expansion.; Grants 1 Governor Governor title.
- Cost: 1410 Culture Icon; 1540
- Requires: Urbanization
- Leads to: Ideology
- Inspiration: Have 3 Corps in your military.
- Policies: Levée en Masse
- obsolete: Conscription
