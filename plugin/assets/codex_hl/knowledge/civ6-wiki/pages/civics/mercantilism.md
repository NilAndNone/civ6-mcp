## Summary

Mercantilism is a Renaissance Era civic in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by earning a Great Merchant.

- Units: Winged Hussar, Privateer, Sea Dog

- Policies: Logistics, Triangular Trade|obsolete = Caravansaries
