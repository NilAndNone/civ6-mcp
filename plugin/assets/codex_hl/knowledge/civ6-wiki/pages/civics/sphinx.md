## Summary

Sphinx
The Sphinx is a unique tile improvement of the Egyptian civilization in Civilization VI. It can be built on any land tile, including Floodplains.

## Key Facts

- unique: Egyptian
- req: Craftsmanship
- Plunder yield: Faith
- effect: +1 Culture;
 +1 additional Culture if built on Floodplains;
+1 ( +2) Appeal to adjacent tiles;
+1 Faith;
+2 Faith if next to a Wonder;
+1 Culture Natural History;
Provides Tourism equal to its Culture output Flight
- terrain: Grassland, Grassland (Hills), Grassland Floodplains, Plains, Plains (Hills), Plains Floodplains, Desert, Desert (Hills), Desert Floodplains, Tundra, Tundra (Hills)
