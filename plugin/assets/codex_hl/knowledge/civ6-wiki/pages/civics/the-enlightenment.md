## Summary

The Enlightenment is a Renaissance Era civic in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by earning 3 Great People of any kind.

## Key Facts

- Era: Renaissance
- Name: The Enlightenment
- notes: Religious Tourism effects are halved versus your civilization.
- Cost: 655 Culture Icon; 720
- Requires: Diplomatic Service
- Leads to: Nationalism, Opera and Ballet
- Inspiration: Earn 3 Great People.
- obsolete: Urban Planning
- Policies: Free Market, Liberalism, Rationalism
- quote: New opinions are always suspected, and usually opposed, without any other reason but because they are not already common.
- quoted: John Locke
- quote1: Whatever is contrary to nature is contrary to reason, and whatsoever is contrary to reason is absurd.
- quoted1: Baruch Spinoza
