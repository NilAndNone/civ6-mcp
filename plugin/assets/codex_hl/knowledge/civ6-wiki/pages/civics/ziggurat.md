## Summary

Ziggurat
The Ziggurat is a unique tile improvement of the Sumerian civilization in Civilization VI. It is available without any technological research.
*Effects:
**+2 Science
**+1 Culture if placed adjacent to a River
**+1 Culture (with Natural History)
*Restrictions:
**Cannot be built on Hills tiles.
