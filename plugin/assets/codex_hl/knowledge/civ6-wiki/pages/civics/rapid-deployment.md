## Summary

Rapid Deployment is an Atomic Era civic in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by building an Aerodrome or an Airstrip on a foreign continent.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Amundsen-Scott Research Station is unlocked with Rapid Deployment. It was unlocked with Cold War in the Rise and Fall expansion.
