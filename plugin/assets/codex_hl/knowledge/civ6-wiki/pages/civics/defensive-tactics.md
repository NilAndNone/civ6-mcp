## Summary

Defensive Tactics is a Classical Era civic in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by receiving a Declaration of War.

## Key Facts

- Era: Classical
- Name: Defensive Tactics
- Cost: 175
- Requires: Games and Recreation, Political Philosophy
- Leads to: Civil Service, Naval Tradition, Feudalism
- Inspiration: Be the target of a Declaration of War.
- Policies: Bastions, Limes
- quote: Invincibility lies in the defense; the possibility of victory in the attack.
- quoted: Sun Tzu
- quote1: Defense is superior to opulence.
- quoted1: Adam Smith
- Infrastructure: Mausoleum at Halicarnassus
- notes: Gain the ability to construct a Spy if playing as Wu Zetian.
 Unlocks two new Casus Belli: War of Reconquest and Protectorate War. Grants 1 Governor Governor title.
