## Summary

Paradise garden
The Pairidaeza is a unique tile improvement of the Persian civilization in Civilization VI.
* Effects:
** +1 Culture
** +2 Gold
** +1 Appeal to adjacent tiles
** +1 Culture for every adjacent Holy Site or Theater Square
** +1 Gold for every adjacent Commercial Hub
** +1 Gold if adjacent to a City Center
** +1 Culture (with Diplomatic Service)
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built on Tundra or Snow tiles or adjacent to another Pairidaeza.
