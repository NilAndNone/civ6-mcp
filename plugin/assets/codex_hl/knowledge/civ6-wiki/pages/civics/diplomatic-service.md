## Summary

Diplomatic Service is a Renaissance Era civic in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by forming an Alliance with another civilization.

## Key Facts

- Era: Renaissance
- Cost: 540 Culture Icon; 600
- Inspiration: Have an alliance with another civilization.
- Requires: Guilds
- Leads to: The Enlightenment
- Policies: Machiavellianism, Wisselbanken
- notes: Allows Resident Embassies and grants the ability to construct a Spy. Four Casus Belli are unlocked: Holy War, War of Liberation, War of Reconquest, and Protectorate War.; +1 Culture to Pairidaezas.
