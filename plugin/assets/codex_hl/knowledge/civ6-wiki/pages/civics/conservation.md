## Summary

Conservation is a Modern Era civic in Civilization VI. It can be hurried by having a Neighborhood district with Breathtaking Appeal.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- Name: Conservation
- Units: Naturalist, Mountie
- Cost: 1255 Culture Icon; 1540
- Inspiration: Have a Neighborhood district with a Breathtaking Appeal.
- Requires: Natural History, Urbanization
- Leads to: Cultural Heritage
- Policies: Resource Management
- notes: Awards 3 Envoys. Allows the building of National Parks and the purchase of a Naturalist with Faith. Allows Builder to plant Woods (second-growth). Woods in your territory that have never been removed (old-growth) gain +1 Appeal. Receive Tourism from Walls.
- quote: Water and air, the two essentials on which life depends, have become global garbage cans.
- quoted: Jacques Yves Cousteau
- quote1: Destroying rainforest for economic gain is like burning a Renaissance painting to cook a meal.
- quoted1: Edward Wilson
- Infrastructure: Sanctuary
