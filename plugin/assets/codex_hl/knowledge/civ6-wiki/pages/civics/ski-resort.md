## Summary

The Ski Resort is a standard tile improvement in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It can only be built on a Mountain tile.
* Effects:
** Provides Tourism equal to the tile's Appeal (4 by default).
** +1 Amenity.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be adjacent to another Ski Resort.
** Cannot be worked or removed.
