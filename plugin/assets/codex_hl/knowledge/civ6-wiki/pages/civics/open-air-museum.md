## Summary

Open-air museum
The Open-Air Museum is a unique tile improvement of the Swedish civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm.
* Effects:
** +2 Loyalty per turn in this city.
** +2 Culture and +2 Tourism for each type of terrain (Snow, Tundra, Desert, Plains, or Grassland) in which at least one Swedish city is founded.
* Restrictions:
** Limit of one per city.
** Tiles with Open-Air Museums cannot be swapped.
