Nuclear weapons, or nukes for short, are immensely powerful, late-game area-of-effect weapons in Civilization VI. To be able to build and use them, players first need to research Nuclear Fission and complete the Manhattan Project (which unlocks the Build Nuclear Device project). Later, in the Information Era, even stronger nukes become available after researching Nuclear Fusion and completing Operation Ivy (which unlocks the Build Thermonuclear Device project).
