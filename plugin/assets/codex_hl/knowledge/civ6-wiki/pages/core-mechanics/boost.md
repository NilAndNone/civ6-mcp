A boost is a brand new feature introduced in Civilization VI, representing a real-life moment of enlightenment which leads to a groundbreaking discovery or invention. The two types of boosts in the game - Eureka Icon Eurekas and Inspiration Icon Inspirations - act as specific ways to progress through the technology and civics tree (respectively) at a much faster rate.
