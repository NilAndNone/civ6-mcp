## Summary

Go to Religion;
Faith is one of the main statistics in Civilization VI. It represents an empire's power in matters of the immaterial and the spiritual world. Faith is used as a currency to fuel a civilization's religious pursuits, similar to how Gold is used as a currency for political functions and civilization upkeep. However, now that we have a whole victory type dedicated to Religion, Faith becomes as important as Gold, if not even more important.
