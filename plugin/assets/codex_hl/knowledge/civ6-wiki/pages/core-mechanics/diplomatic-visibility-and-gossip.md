## Summary

Back to Diplomacy
Visibility Icon Diplomatic Visibility and Gossip Icon Gossip are closely related mechanics introduced in Civilization VI. Unlike prior games, which provided automatic feedback on important happenings in other civilizations, this intel must now be acquired through the Visibility Icon Diplomatic Visibility and Gossip Icon Gossip system.
In short, Visibility Icon Diplomatic Visibility reflects the level of intelligence you can gather about other leaders' agendas, their military plans, and landmark achievements, while Gossip Icon Gossip is the news and messages you receive whenever there are remarkable events occurring in other civilizations.
