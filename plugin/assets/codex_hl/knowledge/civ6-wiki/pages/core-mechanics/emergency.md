Emergency Icon Emergencies are a feature added to Civilization VI in the Rise and Fall expansion pack. They propose a specific mission targeting a specific civilization (usually one which has just done something serious in the game, such as capturing a city). All civs which agree to undertake the mission are bound to succeed or fail within a specific period of time. If they succeed, they will divide a large pot of Gold and get a permanent bonus of some kind; if they fail, the target civ will receive a bonus itself.
