## Summary

Lenses are tools you can use to change your view of the world. Each lens will overlay important information that will help you make strategic decisions throughout the game. There are currently 11 different lenses in the game, 10 of which can be activated manually from the Lenses button above the mini-map, while the other one is activated automatically when you choose to place a District or wonder from a city's Production menu.
