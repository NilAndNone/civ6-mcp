## Summary

The Hall of Fame in Civilization VI is a listing of the best games since you last retired or completed a game. It can be found under "Additional Content" on the game's main menu and is divided into two sections, both of which display information for each ruleset (vanilla, Rise and Fall, and Gathering Storm), scenario, and challenge.
