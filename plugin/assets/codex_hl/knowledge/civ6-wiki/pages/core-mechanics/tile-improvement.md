## Summary

Go to the list of improvements in Civ6
A tile improvement is a modification to a tile created by a unit to improve tile yields, access resources, strengthen an empire's defenses, or provide infrastructure in Civilization VI and its expansions. Tile improvements are the most common way for a civilization, apart from developing its cities, to ensure its advancement in the game.
Note that it is not necessarily better to improve every tile if you don't have the Citizen Population to work them all. Though some improvements can be built on appropriate terrain types without additional resources, it is wise to prioritize the improvement of tiles with bonus, luxury, or strategic resources to gain access to those resources.
