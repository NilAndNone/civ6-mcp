# Speed

## Summary

Speed is a game setting in Civilization VI that modifies the general costs and "scales" of all actions in the game, including the number of turns. It is represented as "Game Speed" on the Game Setup menu.
There are 5 game speeds in Civilization VI:
* Online (200% speed - 250 turns)
* Quick (150% speed - 330 turns)
* Standard (normal speed - 500 turns)
* Epic (66% speed - 750 turns)
* Marathon (33% speed - 1500 turns)
Speed affects:
* All costs of Food (for Citizen Icon Population growth), Production, Gold, Faith, Science, Culture, Influence Points (to get Envoys), and Great People points.
* Most actions that produce lump sum yields, including removing terrain features, harvesting resources, pillaging, clearing Barbarian outposts, and activating Great People. (Exceptions include Gold for using an Apostle with Indulgence Vendor to convert a city.)
* Prices and durations of levying units.
* Prices and durations of most diplomatic agreements and deals.
* Turns for Spies to travel and complete missions.
* Turns for a Barbarian Clan to become a city-state.
* Lifespan Icon Lifespans of Heroes.
* Era Score needed to enter a Golden Age, and not to fall into a Dark Age.
* Strategic resource costs and stockpiles.
* Diplomatic Favor needed to vote in the World Congress.
* How many Light-Years an Exoplanet Expedition must travel to win a Science Victory.
