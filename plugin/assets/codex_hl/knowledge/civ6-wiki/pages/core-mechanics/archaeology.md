In Civilization VI, Archaeology deals with Artifact Artifacts that are extracted from Antiquity Sites and Shipwrecks using Archaeologists and then taken to Archaeological Museums. These Artifact Artifacts can be kept for Culture and Tourism bonuses, or traded with other civilizations.
