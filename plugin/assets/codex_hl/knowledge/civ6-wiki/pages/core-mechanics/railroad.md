## Summary

A railroad is a special type of tile improvement in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It is the ultimate "road" in the game - iron tracks on which fast trains move with speed impossible for even the most modern road network. Railroads accelerate the Moves of land units, allowing them to cross rough terrain and features such as Woods and Rivers easily. All units can move between tiles with railroads at the cost of only 0.25 Moves, and Trade Routes passing through railroads receive a bonus of 0.375 Gold per tile with a railroad.
Unlike roads, however, railroads are not laid automatically by Traders - rather, they must be laid manually by Military Engineers. Placing a railroad on a tile doesn't consume a Build Charges Icon build charge, but it does consume 1 Iron and 1 Coal.
