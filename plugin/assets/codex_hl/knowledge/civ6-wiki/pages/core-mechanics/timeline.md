Timelines are a feature added to Civilization VI in the Rise and Fall expansion pack. They record the various Historic Moments a civilization has achieved, allowing the player to review them later.
Your Timeline can be called up using the button in the upper left corner of the screen. You will see a stylish papyrus where all great achievements of your civilization appear, with the exact year they happened, which game turn it was and the Era Score you earned for them. When you mouse over each particular one you will see a tooltip explaining this score.
