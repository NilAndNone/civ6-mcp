## Summary

War weariness is a game concept and mechanic in Civilization VI and its expansions. It illustrates the public disapproval for the continuation of a prolonged war. It grows for every battle you fight and worsens with casualties.
Much of the information here comes from a CivFanatics info page by Victoria.
