Ages are a feature added to Civilization VI in the Rise and Fall expansion pack. They reflect the general achievements of your civilization for the previous period of its development, bringing enthusiasm and vigor to its people, or otherwise doubt and despair. The new Ages system is reminiscent of the old Golden Age mechanics from previous Civilization titles, but is greatly expanded and intertwined with the new Loyalty mechanics. There are now four distinct Ages (Normal Age Icon Normal, Golden Age Icon Golden, Dark Age Icon Dark and Heroic Age Icon Heroic), each with its own gameplay effects, and instead of them changing only occasionally (as was in Civilization V), Ages now accompany your civilization throughout its history - from the dawn of time to the end of times. Will you remain in a Normal Age, rise to a Golden Age, or fall into a Dark Age? This will depend on what significant feats your civilization performs at any moment.
