## Summary

.]]
A Culture Bomb is a special effect in Civilization VI and its expansions which instantly expands a civilization's territory into nearby neutral, or even foreign-owned territory. Culture bombing is the only way of taking non-neutral territory in the game, barring military conquest or diplomatic negotiations involving switching cities.
