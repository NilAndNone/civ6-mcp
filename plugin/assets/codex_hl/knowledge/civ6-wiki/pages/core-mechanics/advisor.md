## Summary

In Civilization VI and its expansions, the advisor is an in-game entity that helps new players to learn the game and recommends what to build/research. She is voiced by Natasha Loring.
The advisor's recommendations are divided into five categories, each denoted by its own icon:
* Generic
* Culture
* Science
* Faith
* Military
