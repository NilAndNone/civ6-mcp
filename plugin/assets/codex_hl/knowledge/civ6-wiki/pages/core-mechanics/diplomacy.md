## Summary

Go to World Congress (Civ6)
Diplomacy is the art of making relations with other players in Civilization VI. It has been overhauled from previous versions of the game. It's a mechanic that evolves as eras pass, from a state of near constant warfare in ancient times to more civil interactions in the mid- and late game.
