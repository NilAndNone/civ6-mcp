## Summary

The National Park is a special tile improvement in Civilization VI that represents the new environmental drive of your people in the Modern Era.

## Key Facts

- Unique unit: Naturalist, Mountie
- req: Conservation
- effect: Provides Tourism equal to the total Appeal of all the tiles included in it, as well as 2 Amenities to the city that owns it and 1 Amenity to the four closest cities in your empire.
- notes: Occupies 4 tiles (diamond).
