## Summary

Housing is a new concept in Civilization VI, which acts as a slowing factor and eventually a hard limit on Citizen Population growth in each city. It is meant to emulate habitation and sanitation factors in cities, and the limits that overcrowding imposes on growth. Fortunately, there are many different ways that Housing can increase.
