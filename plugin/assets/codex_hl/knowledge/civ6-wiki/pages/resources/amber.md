## Summary

AmberBaltic amber
Amber is a luxury resource in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It is found on Woods, Rainforest, Coast, and Lake tiles.
* +1 Culture
* +1 Science if found in a water tile with an Aquarium
* +1 Production if found in a water tile with a Stave Church
* +4 Amenities (+1 Amenity to up to 4 cities)
