## Summary

Aluminium
Aluminum is an Atomic Era Strategic Resource in Civilization VI. It is revealed with Radio.
Aluminum is found on Desert, Desert Hills, and Plains tiles. In the Gathering Storm expansion, it is also found on Rainforest tiles.
* +1 Science
