## Summary

Go to List of resources in Civ6
A resource is one of the special commodities found in limited quantities on the map in Civilization VI and its expansions. As in all Civilization games, they are essential to your empire's development and are an important reason to seize and hold territory. Different resources may be found virtually anywhere except Ocean tiles. There is a limit of one resource per tile.
