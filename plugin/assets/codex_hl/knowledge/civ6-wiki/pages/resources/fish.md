## Summary

Fish
Fish are a Bonus Resource in Civilization VI. They are found on Coast, Lake, and Reef tiles.
* +1 Food.
* +1 Science with an Aquarium
* +1 Production with a Stave Church
* May be Harvested for Food (requires Celestial Navigation)
