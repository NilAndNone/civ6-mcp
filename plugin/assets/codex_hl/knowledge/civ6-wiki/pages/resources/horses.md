## Summary

Horse
Horses are an Ancient Era Strategic Resource in Civilization VI. In vanilla Civilization VI and Rise and Fall, they are the only resource of their type to be revealed at the beginning of the game; in Gathering Storm, they are revealed with Animal Husbandry.
Horses may be found on flatland Grassland and Plains tiles.
* +1 Food
* +1 Production
