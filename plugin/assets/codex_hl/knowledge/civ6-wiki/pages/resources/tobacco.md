## Summary

Tobacco
Tobacco is a luxury resource in Civilization VI. It is fairly common, and can be found on Grassland and Plains tiles, and also in both Woods and Rainforests.
* +1 Faith
* +4 Amenities (+1 Amenity to up to 4 cities)
