## Summary

Pearl
Pearls are a luxury resource in Civilization VI. They are found in Coast and Lake tiles.
* +1 Faith
* +1 Science with an Aquarium
* +1 Production with a Stave Church
* +4 Amenities (+1 Amenity to up to 4 cities)
