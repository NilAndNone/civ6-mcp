## Summary

Crab
Crabs are a Bonus Resource in Civilization VI. They are found in Coast and Lake tiles.
* +2 Gold
* +1 Science with an Aquarium
* +1 Production with a Stave Church
* May be Harvested for Gold (requires Celestial Navigation)
