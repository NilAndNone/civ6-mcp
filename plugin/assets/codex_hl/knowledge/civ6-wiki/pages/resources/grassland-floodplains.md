## Summary

Grassland Floodplains are a terrain feature in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. They are found on flat Grassland tiles near Rivers.
* Yields:
** Nothing; may be enhanced by river flooding disasters.
* Defense provided:
** -2 penalty
* Additional traits:
** Subject to floodings; they may damage or destroy anything built on them, but may also increase their base yields.
** -1 Appeal
** Can accept Districts and wonders
