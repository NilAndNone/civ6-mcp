## Summary

Kublai Khan
Kublai Khan (23 September 1215 – 18 February 1294), also known as Emperor Shizu of Yuan, was the fifth Great Khan of the Mongols, ruling from 1260 until his death. He also founded the Yuan dynasty in 1271 after his conquest of China, ruling as its first emperor. He leads both the Chinese and the Mongolians in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall.
Kublai Khan, unlike his grandfather, focuses on establishing Trade Routes and using the accompanying Eurekas and Inspirations to fuel a Science Victory.
