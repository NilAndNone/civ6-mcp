## Summary

Jayavarman VII
Jayavarman VII (c. 1123 – c. 1218) was king of the Khmer empire from approximately 1181 until his death, best known for significantly fostering the spread of Buddhism in Cambodia, as well as for his prolific construction of temples, hospitals, highways and reservoirs throughout his kingdom. He leads the Khmer in Civilization VI.
Jayavarman's Khmer Empire features populous cities of devout Buddhists, especially those along rivers where all their abilities can come into play.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Khmer and Indonesia Civilization & Scenario Pack
- Civilization: Khmer
- Leader ability: Monasteries of the King
- Leader ability description: Holy Sites grant Food equal to their adjacency bonus, receive a +2 adjacency bonus from Rivers, grant +2 Housing if built next to a River, and trigger a Culture Bomb when built, claiming surrounding tiles.
- Agenda: An End to Suffering
- Agenda description: Likes civilizations with many Holy Sites and a high average city Citizen Population. Dislikes civilizations lacking in either of these areas.
- religion: Buddhism
- quote: Provide for your people. To do less is to be less.
