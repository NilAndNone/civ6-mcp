## Summary

Hōjō Tokimune
Hojo Tokimune (5 June 1251 – 20 April 1284) was the eighth shikken of the Kamakura shogunate, ruling from 1268 until his death, best known for twice repelling the Mongols in their attempted invasions of Japan, as well as for fostering the spread of Zen Buddhism through the samurai class. He leads the Japanese in Civilization VI.
With Hojo in charge, the "way of the warrior" for Japan is to build a nice, compact coastal empire and mix in religious and cultural development with military advancement.
