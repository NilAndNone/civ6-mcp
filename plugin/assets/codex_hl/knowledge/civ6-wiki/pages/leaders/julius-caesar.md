## Summary

Julius Caesar
Gaius Julius Caesar (12 July 100 BC – 15 March 44 BC) was a prominent Roman general and statesman. Considered one of history's greatest military commanders, he led the Roman armies to victory in the Gallic Wars and, after defeating his rival Pompey in a civil war, seized power and declared himself dictator, starting a programme of radical political reforms until his assassination in 44 BC. He leads the Romans in Civilization VI.
Julius Caesar wants to conquer every hex of the map, vanquishing both Barbarians and opposing civilizations.
