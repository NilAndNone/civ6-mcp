## Summary

Mansa Musa
Musa I (c. 1280 – 1337), better known as Mansa Musa, was the ninth mansa of the Mali Empire. Widely considered to have been the wealthiest person in history (some sources measuring his wealth at around $400 billion when adjusted for inflation), his vast wealth was used to attract scholars, merchants and architects to Mali, establishing it as a beacon of Islamic trade, culture and learning. He leads the Malians in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm.
The wealth of Mansa Musa will flow through his cities creating a mighty desert empire.
