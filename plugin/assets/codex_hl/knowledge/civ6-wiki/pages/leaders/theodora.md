## Summary

Theodora (wife of Justinian I)
Theodora (c. 500 – 28 June 548) was the wife of emperor Justinian, ruling as empress from 527 until her death. As her husband's closest and most trusted advisor, she held considerable influence over Byzantium, and has been recognized as one of the most powerful women in Byzantine history. She leads the Byzantines in Civilization VI.
Empress Theodora brings the same devotion to Faith and Religion as you needed to rise from your humble beginnings.
