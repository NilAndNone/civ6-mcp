Qin Shi Huang
Qin Shi Huang (18 February 260 – 10 September 210 BC), born Ying Zheng, was the first emperor of China and the founder of the Qin dynasty. Originally the king of the state of Qin, he put an end to the Warring States period through a series of military campaigns that led to the unification of China. He leads the Chinese in Civilization VI.
Qin is the first among emperors and therefore he needs to be first in amazing wonders built for his legacy.
This is the standard version of Qin Shi Huang when players do not have the Rulers of China Pack enabled. When it is enabled, Qin Shi Huang will be split into two alternate personas: Unifier Qin Shi Huang, with a new appearance and abilities; and Mandate of Heaven Qin Shi Huang, using his former model and abilities.
