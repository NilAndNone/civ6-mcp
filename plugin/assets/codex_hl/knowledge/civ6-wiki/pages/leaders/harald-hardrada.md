Harald Hardrada
Harald III (c. 1015 – 25 September 1066), also known as Harald Sigurdsson or by his epithet Hardrada (meaning "hard ruler"), was king of Norway from 1046 until his death, also known for leading the Byzantine Varangian Guard, and for his invasion and attempted takeovers of Denmark and England. He leads the Norwegians in Civilization VI.
Hardrada is a naval power like England and Spain, though he strives to dominate the seas as a springboard for his quick Norwegian strike on coastal targets.
This is the standard version of Harald Hardrada when players do not have the Rulers of England Pack enabled. When it is enabled, Harald will be split into two alternate personas: Varangian Harald Hardrada, with a new appearance and abilities; and Konge Harald Hardrada, using his former model and abilities.
