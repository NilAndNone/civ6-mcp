## Summary

Tomyris
Tomyris (? – c. 520s BCE) was a Saka warrior-queen who reigned over the Massagetae, a Scythian pastoral-nomadic confederation in Central Asia. She is known for leading her people against an invasion by Cyrus the Great's Achaemenid Empire, allegedly defeating and killing him in 530 BC. She leads the Scythians in Civilization VI.
Tomyris' Scythia is going to be a threat to strike quickly and decisively at any time, especially during the Ancient and Classical periods. Luckily she has a strict code of honor and will never backstab anyone; she'll only be attacking those who deserve it for mistreating others.
