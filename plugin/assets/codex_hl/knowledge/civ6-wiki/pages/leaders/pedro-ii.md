## Summary

Pedro II of Brazil
Dom Pedro II (2 December 1825 – 5 December 1891), nicknamed the Magnanimous, was the second and last emperor of Brazil, renowned for his vast education and sponsoring of learning, culture and science, as well as for his steadfast push towards the definitive abolition of slavery in Brazil. He leads the Brazilians in Civilization VI.
As much as Qin Shi Huang covets wonders, Pedro covets Great People.
