## Summary

John III of Portugal
João III (7 June 1502 – 11 June 1557), nicknamed the Pious, was king of Portugal from 1521 until his death. Known for his expansion of the Portuguese Empire, he started the the colonization of Brazil and secured control of the Maluku Islands, ensuring Portuguese monopoly over the spice trade. He leads the Portuguese in Civilization VI.
Portugal seeks to explore the map to set the stage for a sea-based trade empire, taking advantage of its advantages in coastal resources and nautical exploration.
