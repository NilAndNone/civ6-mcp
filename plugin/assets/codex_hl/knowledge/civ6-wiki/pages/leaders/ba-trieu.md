## Summary

Lady Triệu
Triệu Thị Trinh (8 November 226 – 4 April 248), better known by the honorific Bà Triệu or Lady Triệu, was a Vietnamese warrior who led an uprising against the Eastern Wu occupation of Vietnam, and afterwards became a popular and highly celebrated folk hero in Vietnamese culture. She leads the Vietnamese in Civilization VI.
Bà Triệu uses her bonuses to unit speed and cheaper Thành districts for an effective defense as she acquires Culture – or as a base from which to launch a Domination Victory.

## Key Facts

- Leader ability description: All units gain +5 Strength Combat Strength when fighting in Rainforest, Marsh, and Woods tiles, and +1 Movement if they start their turn there. Both of these bonuses are doubled in tiles inside Vietnamese territory.

- Agenda: Defender of the Homeland
- Agenda description: Likes civilizations that have never declared war on Vietnam. She will never forgive those who have, with her opinion decreasing further with each turn the war lasts, and this decreased opinion never recovers.
