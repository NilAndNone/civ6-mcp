## Summary

Frederick Barbarossa
Frederick I (1122 – 10 June 1199), better known as Frederick Barbarossa (meaning "red beard"), a nickname given to him by the Italians he spent most of his rule warring with, was king of Germany and Holy Roman Emperor from 1155 until his death. His many political and military accomplishments made him a legendary figure both during and after his time, and one of the most celebrated Holy Roman Emperors. He leads the Germans in Civilization VI.
As in history, Germany under Frederick Barbarossa is a powerhouse that can threaten to overwhelm its foes with their productivity and military prowess.
