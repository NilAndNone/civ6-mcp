## Summary

Peter the Great
Peter I (9 June 1672 – 8 February 1725), more commonly known as Peter the Great, was tsar of Russia from 1682 until his death. During his reign, he greatly expanded the borders of the Russian Empire, turning it into a major political player in Europe, and modernized Russia's traditional culture and politics through the introduction of Western-style reforms inspired by the ideas of the Enlightenment. He leads the Russians in Civilization VI.
Peter's Russia is the spreading amoeba you don't want next door. He is great at absorbing all the land, technologies, and civics around him!
