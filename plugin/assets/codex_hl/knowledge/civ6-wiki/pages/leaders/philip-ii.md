## Summary

Philip II of Spain
Philip II (21 May 1527 – 13 September 1598), nicknamed the Prudent, was king of Spain, Portugal, Naples and Sicily, Duke of Milan, Lord of the Netherlands, and briefly king consort of England through his marriage to Mary I. His reign, during which the Spanish Empire reached the height of its expansion and power and experienced a flourishing of art and literature, is considered the Spanish Golden Age. He leads the Spanish in Civilization VI.
Philip leads another global naval empire, but unlike England, this one is the leading candidate to win a Religious Victory.
