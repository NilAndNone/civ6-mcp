## Summary

Simón Bolívar
Simón Bolívar (unit) (Civ6)
Simón José Antonio de la Santísima Trinidad Bolívar Palacios Ponte y Blanco (24 July 1783 – 17 December 1830), better known as Simón Bolívar and nicknamed el Libertador, was a Venezuelan general and statesman who led much of South America to independence from Spanish control during the Spanish American wars of independence, afterwards becoming president of Gran Colombia, Peru, and Bolivia. He leads the Gran Colombians in Civilization VI.
Simón Bolívar's units are a nimble force to be reckoned with, whether on offense or defense. With clever use of his many Comandante Generals, his army becomes nigh unstoppable.
