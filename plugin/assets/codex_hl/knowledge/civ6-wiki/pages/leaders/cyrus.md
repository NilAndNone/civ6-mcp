## Summary

Cyrus the Great
Cyrus II (c. 600 – 530 BC), commonly known as Cyrus the Great, was the founder of the Achaemenid Empire, the largest empire in the world up to that point, stretching from the Mediterranean in the west to the Indus river in the east. His policies of religious and cultural tolerance and a degree of autonomy for local rulers allowed the empire to maintain stability despite its vast territory and multi-ethnic composition. He leads the Persians in Civilization VI.
Cyrus looks for juicy opportunities to expand his empire with quick strike surprise wars. Once captured, this new territory can be rapidly integrated into the Persian network of cities connected by a highly efficient road network.
