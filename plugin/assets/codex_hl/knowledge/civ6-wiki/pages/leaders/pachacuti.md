## Summary

Pachacuti
Pachacuti (1410 AD – 1471 or 1472 AD), born Cusi Yupanqui, was the ninth Sapa Inca of Cusco. Through a combination of military campaigns and skilled diplomacy, he conducted a swift and far-reaching expansion of his kingdom that led to the establishment of the Inca Empire. He leads the Incas in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm.
Pachacuti builds Terrace Farms and Qhapaq Ñan to grow sprawling mountain cities.
