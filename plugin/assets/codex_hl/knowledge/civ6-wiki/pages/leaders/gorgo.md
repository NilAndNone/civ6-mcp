## Summary

Gorgo, Queen of Sparta
Gorgo (c. 513 – 429 BC) was a queen of Sparta, daughter of king Cleomenes I, wife of king Leonidas I and mother to king Pleistarchus. She is notably one of the very few women to be named by ancient historians like Herodotus and Plutarch, being noted for her intelligence, wisdom and judgment. She leads the Greeks in Civilization VI.
Who better to be master of the civic side of life than the Greeks? As a Spartan, Gorgo can use the power and flexibility of the civics system to set up for any kind of victory.
