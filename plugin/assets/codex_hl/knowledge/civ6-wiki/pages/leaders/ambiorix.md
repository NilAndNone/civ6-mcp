## Summary

Ambiorix
Ambiorix (? – c. 53 BC) was a king of the Gallic Eburones tribe in northeastern Gaul, nowadays Belgium, who became infamous during the Gallic Wars for leading a revolt against the Roman forces commanded by Julius Caesar, wiping out fifteen Roman cohorts before finally being defeated. He leads the Gauls in Civilization VI.
Ambiorix builds a swarm of units to overwhelm his foes via strategic construction of mines and his unique Oppidum district.
