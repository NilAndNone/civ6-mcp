## Summary

Basil II
Basil II (c. 958 – 15 December 1025), nicknamed the Bulgar-Slayer for his subjugation of the Bulgars, was the senior Byzantine emperor from 976 to his death. His 49-year reign, the longest of any Byzantine emperor, is considered the pinnacle of Byzantine power during the Middle Ages. He leads the Byzantines in Civilization VI.
Byzantium combines religious and military tactics towards a Domination or Religious Victory.
