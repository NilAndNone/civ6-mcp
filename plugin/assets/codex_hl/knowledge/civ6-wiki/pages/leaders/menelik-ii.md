## Summary

Menelik II
Menelik II (17 August 1844 – 12 December 1913), baptized as Sahle Maryam, was emperor of Ethiopia from 1889 until his death. Renowned for his expansion and modernization of Ethiopia, his victory in the First Italo-Ethiopian War secured Ethiopia's independence from European imperialism. He leads the Ethiopians in Civilization VI.
Menelik founds cities on the hills, focusing on Faith and letting his Council of Ministers boost his Science and Culture with an eye towards a Religious or Culture Victory.
