## Summary

Moctezuma I
Montezuma I (c. 1398 AD – 1469 AD) was the fifth tlatoani of Tenochtitlan and second huey tlatoani of the Aztec Empire. He is known for greatly expanding Aztec territory, as well as consolidating Tenochtitlan's supremacy as the dominant partner over Texcoco and Tlacopan in the Aztec Triple Alliance. He leads the Aztecs in Civilization VI.
Montezuma is an aggressive expansionist who pushes out his empire in order to collect all the Luxuries he can.
