## Summary

Wilhelmina of the Netherlands
Wilhelmina (31 August 1880 – 28 November 1962) was queen of the Netherlands from 1890 until her abdication in 1948, the longest reign of any Dutch monarch, and is best remembered for her staunch support of the Dutch resistance during the Nazi occupation of the Netherlands. She leads the Dutch in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall.
Wilhelmina's Dutch empire is at home on the flatlands where rivers run into the sea, building polders to fuel their growth and trade routes to span the globe.
