## Summary

Wilfrid Laurier
Wilfrid Laurier (20 November 1841 – 17 February 1919) was the seventh Prime Minister of Canada, in office from 1896 to 1911, the longest uninterrupted term of any Canadian Prime Minister. He is best remembered for his policy of conciliation between French and English Canada, his construction of new transcontinental railways and his encouragement of the settling of the Canadian West. He leads the Canadians in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm.
Laurier negotiates through diplomatic crisis to protect his Tundra empire.
