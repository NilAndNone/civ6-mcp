Victorian (Civ6)
Queen Victoria
Victoria (24 May 1819 – 22 January 1901) was queen of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland from 1837 until her death, as well as empress of India since 1876. Her 63-year reign, one of the longest in history, saw the British Empire achieve the height of its expansion, as well as experience sweeping economic, cultural and political transformations, and became known as the Victorian Era in her honor. She leads the English in Civilization VI.
Victoria is a polite and gentle young lady as long as she is getting her way. But if you don't let her on your continent, she is not amused!
This is the standard version of Victoria when players do not have the Rulers of England Pack enabled. When it is enabled, Victoria will be split into two alternate personas: Age of Steam Victoria, with a new appearance and abilities, and Age of Empire Victoria, using her former model and abilities.
