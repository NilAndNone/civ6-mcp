## Summary

Gilgamesh
Gilgamesh is the main character of the Epic of Gilgamesh, an Akkadian poem based on earlier Sumerian sources that is considered the oldest known work of literature in history, which describes him as the demigod king of the city-state of Uruk. The general consensus is that the character was likely based on a historical king of Uruk that reigned sometime between 2800 and 2500 BC and was later deified. He leads the Sumerians in Civilization VI.
Gilgamesh is more valuable as an ally than any other leader in the game.
