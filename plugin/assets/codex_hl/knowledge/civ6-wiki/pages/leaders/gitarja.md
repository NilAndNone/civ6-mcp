## Summary

Tribhuwana Wijayatunggadewi
Dyah Gitarja (c. 1309 – ?), regnal name Tribhuwannottunggadewi Jayawishnuwardhani, was a Javanese queen regent and the third monarch of the Majapahit empire, ruling from 1328 until 1350, when she abdicated in favour of her son Hayam Wuruk. During her reign, with the assistance of her prime minister Gajah Mada, she achieved a significant expansion of the Majapahit empire's territory and influence. She leads the Indonesians in Civilization VI.
Gitarja is the queen of all the islands and coasts, with cities spreading out onto the water and protected by Indonesian Jongs.
