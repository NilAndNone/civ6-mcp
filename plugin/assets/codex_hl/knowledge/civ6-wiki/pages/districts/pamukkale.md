## Summary

Pamukkale
Pamukkale is a two-tile impassable natural wonder available in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. Cities that own at least one wonder tile receive +1 Amenity, or +2 Amenities if an Entertainment Complex is built adjacent to the wonder. Pamukkale also provides a major adjacency bonus to Campuses, Theater Squares, and Commercial Hubs and an extra standard adjacency bonus (+3 total) to Holy Sites.
Each Pamukkale tile provides fresh water and +2 Appeal to adjacent tiles.
Discovering Pamukkale grants +1 Era Score, or +3 Score if the player is the first to do so.
