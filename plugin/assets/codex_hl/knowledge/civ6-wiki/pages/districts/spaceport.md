## Summary

The Spaceport is a District in Civilization VI, dedicated to conquering space. It requires discovery of the Rocketry technology and cannot be built on Hills.
* Effects:
** Allows development of the Space Race projects, which are the way to Science Victory.
** Does not depend on Population Population.
