## Summary

The Canal is a District in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm that provides passage from a body of water to a City Center or another body of water. It requires Steam Power (or Masonry for Mandate of Heaven Qin Shi Huang).
The Canal must be built on flat land with a Coast or Lake tile on one side, and either a City Center or another body of water on the other. A single canal passage may go either straight, or bend 60 degrees; three-way canals are not allowed.
* Effects:
** Creates a maritime route, allowing ships to pass through it.
** Allows Traders to pass through it as part of their maritime Trade Routes. Trade Routes passing through a Canal receive bonus Gold.
** +1 Amenity with Water Works.
** Does not depend on Population Population.
** No limit on the number that can be built per city.
** Military Engineers can spend a charge to complete 20% (rounded down) of a Canal's Production cost.
** +1 Appeal
