## Summary

The Government Plaza is a specialty District in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall, dedicated to administrative and bureaucratic affairs. It requires the State Workforce civic.
* Effects:
** +8 Loyalty to this city.
** +1 adjacency bonus to all adjacent districts.
** Awards +1 Governor Icon Governor Title.
* Restrictions:
** Limit of one per civilization.
