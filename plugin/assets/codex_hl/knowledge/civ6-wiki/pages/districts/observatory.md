## Summary

El Caracol, Chichen ItzaObservatory
The Observatory is a unique District of the Mayan civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Campus.
* Effects:
** Lower Production cost (27 vs. 54).
** Major bonus (+2 Science) for every adjacent Plantation.
** Minor bonus (+½ Science) for every adjacent Farm or district.
** Major bonus (+2 Science) for each adjacent Great Barrier Reef tile.
** Major bonus (+2 Science) for each adjacent Pamukkale tile.
** +1 Scientist point per turn.
** Specialists add +2 Science each
