## Summary

The Dam is a District in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm, dedicated to improving the comfort and safety of people living along a River. It must be built on a Floodplains tile and the River must traverse at least 2 adjacent sides of the future Dam tile.
* Effects:
** +3 Housing
** +1 Amenity from entertainment
** +1 Amenity with Water Works
** +1 Appeal to adjacent tiles
** Prevents damage from Floods on this River.
** Reduces yields from Floods (Food and Production bonuses) by 50%.
** Prevents tiles' Food loss during Droughts for this city.
** Acts as a bridge over its River.
** Does not depend on Population Population. A single city can construct as many Dams as its territory covers different Rivers with applicable Floodplains tiles.
** Military Engineers can spend a charge to complete 20% (rounded down) of a Dam's Production cost.
* Restrictions:
** Limit of one per River. If multiple Dams are being constructed on the same River, only the first Dam to be completed will stay.
