## Summary

Thermae
The Bath is a unique District of the Roman civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Aqueduct and must be built adjacent to both the City Center and a River, Lake, Oasis, or Mountain.
* Effects:
** Lower Production cost (18 vs. 36).
** +1 Amenity
** +2 Housing
** Provides Housing based on whether or not the city has fresh water.
*** +6 Housing for landlocked cities without fresh water.
*** +5 Housing for coastal cities without fresh water.
*** +4 Housing for cities with fresh water.
** +2 Housing with Water Works.
** Prevents Food loss during droughts.
** +1 Amenity if adjacent to a Geothermal Fissure.
** Military Engineers can spend a charge to complete 20% (rounding down) of an Bath's Production cost.
** Does not depend on Population Population.
