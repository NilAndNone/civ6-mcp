## Summary

Brazilian Carnival
The Street Carnival is a unique District of the Brazilian civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Entertainment Complex.
* Effects:
** Lower Production cost (27 vs. 54).
** +2 Amenities from entertainment to parent city.
** Amenities from the Zoo and Stadium buildings extend to cities whose City Centers are up to 6 tiles away from the district.
** +1 Appeal to adjacent tiles
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built if a Copacabana has already been built in this city.
