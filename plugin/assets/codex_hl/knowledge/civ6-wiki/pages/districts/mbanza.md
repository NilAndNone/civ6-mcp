## Summary

Mbanza
The Mbanza is a unique District of the Kongolese civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Neighborhood, must be built on Woods or Rainforest tiles and is unlocked with Guilds.
* Effects:
** Lower Production cost (27 vs. 54).
** +2 Food
** +4 Gold
** +5 Housing
** +2 Housing with Water Works.
** Yield does not change based on the tile's Appeal.
** Does not depend on Population Population.
