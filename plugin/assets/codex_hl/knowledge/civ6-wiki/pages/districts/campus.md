## Summary

The Campus is a specialty District in Civilization VI, dedicated to Science and technological advancement. It requires the Writing technology.

## Key Facts

- Era: Ancient
- Required technology/civic: Writing
- Plunder yield: Science
- Production cost: 54
- Maintenance: 1
- Great person points: +1 Scientist point per turn.
- Adjacency bonus: +2 Science from each adjacent Great Barrier Reef tile;
 +2 Science from each adjacent Pamukkale tile;
 +2 Science from each adjacent Geothermal Fissure and Reef;
+1 Science from each adjacent Mountain;
+1 Science from every 2 adjacent Rainforest and district tiles
- citizen yields (per citizen): +2 Science
- trade yields: Domestic Destination: +1 Food. ; International Destination: +1 Science.
- unlocks buildings: Library,University,Research Lab
- unlocks projects: Campus Research Grants,Occult Research,Dark Signal
- uniquevariants: Observatory,Mayan,Seowon,Korean
