# Industrial Zone

- Category: districts
- Source: [https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Zone_%28Civ6%29](https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Zone_%28Civ6%29)

- Local game IDs: DISTRICT_INDUSTRIAL_ZONE

## Summary

The Industrial Zone is a specialty District in Civilization VI, dedicated to production and industry. It requires Apprenticeship.

## Key Facts

- Era: Medieval
- Great person points: +1 engineer point per turn.
- Adjacency bonus: +1 Production from each adjacent Quarry. ; +1 Production from each adjacent strategic resource. ; +1 Production from every 2 adjacent district tiles. ; +1 Production from every 2 adjacent Mine or Lumber Mill. ; +2 Production from every Aqueduct, Bath, Canal or Dam.
- citizen yields (per citizen): +2 Production
- trade yields: Domestic Destination: +1 Production;
International Destination: +1 Production
- Production cost: 54
- Maintenance: 1
- appeal: -1
- unlocks buildings: Workshop,Factory,Power Plant,Coal Power Plant,Oil Power Plant,Nuclear Power Plant
- unlocks projects: Industrial Zone Logistics,Convert to Coal Power,Convert to Oil Power, Convert to Nuclear Power,Recommission Nuclear Reactor,Carbon Recapture,Decommission Coal Power Plant,Decommission Oil Power Plant,Decommission Nuclear Power Plant
- Required technology/civic: Apprenticeship
- Plunder yield: Science
- uniquevariants: Hansa,German,Oppidum,Gallic
