## Summary

Imperial Citadel of Thăng Long
The Thành is a unique District of the Vietnamese civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Encampment.
* Effects:
** Lower Production cost (27 vs. 54)
** +2 Culture for every adjacent District
**Provides Tourism from Culture adjacency bonus after researching Flight
** Is not considered a specialty District and does not require Population Population
**Provides no General point
** Acquires Outer Defenses and Ranged Strike along with the City Center once Walls have been built
** Blocks movement of foreign units to this tile, unless the District is pillaged
** Spawns all land military units the city produces or purchases
** Specialists provide +1 Production and 2 Gold each
**Spies operate at one level lower when targeting this city with Consulate
** Gives its parent city the ability to build land units with only 1 count of the relative Strategic Resource (Vanilla and only)
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built next to the City Center
