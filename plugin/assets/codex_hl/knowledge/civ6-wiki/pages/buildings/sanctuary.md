## Summary

The Sanctuary is an advanced conservation building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Preserve district.
* Effects:
** +1 Science and Gold to adjacent unimproved tiles with Charming Appeal
** Yields increased to +2 Science, Gold and Production for adjacent unimproved tiles with Breathtaking Appeal
