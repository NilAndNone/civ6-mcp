## Summary

List of fortifications in Georgia (country)
The Tsikhe is a unique building of the Georgian civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It is built in the City Center and replaces the Renaissance Walls.
* Effects:
** {{PositiveText|Lower Production cost (260 vs. 305 ( 300)).}}
** +4 Faith.
** +200 Outer Defense Strength.
** +100% Tourism and Faith while in a Golden Age
** +3 Strength Combat Strength (total of +9 Strength Combat Strength from previous Walls)
** +1 Housing (with Monarchy, total of +3 Housing from previous Walls)
** +2 Diplomatic Favor (with Monarchy)
** Becomes obsolete with Steel, which automatically builds defenses in every city.
** Cannot be purchased with Gold.
** +3 Tourism (with Conservation, total of +6 Tourism from previous Walls and +9 Tourism in a Golden Age)
** +2 Science with Military Research policy card
