## Summary

The Intelligence Agency is a Tier 2 government building in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It is built in the Government Plaza district and requires a Tier 2 government (Merchant Republic, Monarchy, or Theocracy) and a Tier 1 government building (Ancestral Hall, Audience Chamber, or Warlord's Throne).
* Effects:
** +1 Spy and Spy capacity.
** All Spy Operations have a higher chance of success.
** Awards +1 Governor Icon Governor Title.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built if Foreign Ministry, Grand Master's Chapel, or Queen's Bibliotheque has already been built in this district.
