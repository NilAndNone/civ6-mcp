## Summary

The Royal Society is a Tier 3 government building in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It is built in the Government Plaza district and requires a Tier 3 government (Communism, Democracy, or Fascism) and a Tier 2 government building (Foreign Ministry, Grand Master's Chapel, Intelligence Agency, or Queen's Bibliotheque).
* Effects:
**Builders gain the ability to use all their charges to provide bonus Production to a District Project (2% of the project's Production cost per Build Charges Icon build charge used). Once per city per turn.
** Awards +1 Governor Icon Governor Title.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built if National History Museum or War Department has already been built in this district.
