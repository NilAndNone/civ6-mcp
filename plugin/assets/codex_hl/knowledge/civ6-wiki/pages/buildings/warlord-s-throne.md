## Summary

The Warlord's Throne is a Tier 1 government building in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It is built in the Government Plaza district and requires a Tier 1 government (Autocracy, Classical Republic, or Oligarchy).
* Effects:
** Capturing an enemy City grants 20% bonus Production in all Cities for 5 turns.
** Awards +1 Governor Icon Governor Title.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built if Ancestral Hall or Audience Chamber has already been built in this district.
