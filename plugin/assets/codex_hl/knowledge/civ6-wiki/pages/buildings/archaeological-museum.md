## Summary

The Archaeological Museum is an advanced cultural building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Theater Square district and requires an Amphitheater (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +2 Culture (boosted by Grand Opera policy card)
** +1 Citizen Icon Citizen slot
** +1 Writer point per turn
** +2 Artist points per turn
** +2 Culture for each Cultural City-State with 6 Envoys.
*** With Ethiopia Pack: +2 Culture for each Cultural City-State with 3 Envoys.
** +3 Artifact Icon Artifact slots (+6 if playing as England in vanilla Civilization VI and Rise and Fall)
*** Theming bonus: Doubles Tourism output when displaying Artifact Icon Artifacts of the same era from different civilizations
** Allows training and maintaining 1 Archaeologist, while there are free Artifact Icon Artifact slots in the Museum
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built if Art Museum has already been built in this district
