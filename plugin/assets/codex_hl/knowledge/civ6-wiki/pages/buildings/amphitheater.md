## Summary

The Amphitheater is a basic cultural building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Theater Square district (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +2 Culture (boosted by Grand Opera policy card)
** +1 Citizen Icon Citizen slot
** +1 Writer point per turn
** +2 Writing Icon Great Work of Writing slots
** +2 Culture for each Cultural City-State with 3 Envoys.
*** With Ethiopia Pack: +1 Culture for each Cultural City-State with 1 Envoy.
