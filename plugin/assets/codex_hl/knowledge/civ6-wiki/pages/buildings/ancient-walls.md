## Summary

The Ancient Walls are a basic defensive building in Civilization VI. They are built in the City Center and require Masonry.
* Effects:
** Provides Outer Defense for the city and the Encampment (if available) that must be depleted before the city itself is assaulted.
** +100 Outer Defense.
** +3 Strength Combat Strength
** Becomes obsolete after Steel, which automatically builds defenses in every city.
** +1 Housing (with Monarchy)
** +1 Tourism (with Conservation)
