## Summary

Madrasa
The Madrasa is a unique building of the Arabian civilization in Civilization VI. It is built in the Campus district, replaces the University and is unlocked with Theology.
* Effects:
** +5 Science (boosted by Rationalism policy card)
** Provides bonus Faith equal to the adjacency Science bonus of its district
** +1 Housing
** +1 Citizen Citizen slot
** +1 Scientist point per turn
** +2 Science for each Scientific City-State with 6 Envoys.
*** With Ethiopia Pack: +2 Science for each Scientific City-State with 3 Envoys.
** +2 Science with Great Scientist Isaac Newton activated.
