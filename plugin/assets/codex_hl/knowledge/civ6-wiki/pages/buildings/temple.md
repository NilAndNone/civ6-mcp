## Summary

The Temple is an advanced religious building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Holy Site district and requires a Shrine (or one of its replacements).

## Key Facts

- Name: Temple
- Era: Classical
- Cost: 120
- reqinfrastructure: Shrine
- effect: +4 Faith;
+1 Citizen Citizen slot;
+1 Prophet point per turn.;
+1 Relic Relic slot;
Allows the purchasing of Apostles, Gurus, and Inquisitors (after an Apostle uses its Launch Inquisition ability) with Faith.
