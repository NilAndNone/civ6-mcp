## Summary

Marae
The Marae is a unique building of the Māori civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It is built in the Theater Square district and replaces the Amphitheater.
* Effects:
** +1 Culture and Faith to all of this city's tiles with a passable feature.
** +1 Tourism to all of this city's tiles with a feature (with Flight).
** +2 Culture for each Cultural City-State with 3 Envoys.
*** With Ethiopia Pack: +1 Culture for each Cultural City-State with 1 Envoy.
** No maintenance cost.
** No Great Work slots.
