## Summary

The Workshop is a basic production building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Industrial Zone district (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +2 Production
*** +3 Production
** +1 Citizen Citizen slot
** +1 Engineer point per turn
** +3 Culture for each time Great Engineer Leonardo da Vinci activated.
** +2 Production toward wonders, buildings, and districts for each Industrial City-State with 3 Envoys.
*** With Ethiopia Pack: +1 Production toward wonders, buildings, and districts for each Industrial City-State with 1 Envoy.
