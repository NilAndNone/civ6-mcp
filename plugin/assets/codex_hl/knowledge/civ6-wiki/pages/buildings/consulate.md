## Summary

The Consulate is a basic diplomatic building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Diplomatic Quarter district.
* Effects:
** +2 Influence Points per turn.
** Spies operate at one level lower when targeting this city or cities with Encampments.
** +2 Production toward units for each Militaristic City-State with 3 Envoys.
** +2 Production toward wonders, buildings, and districts for each Industrial City-State with 3 Envoys.
** +2 Science for each Scientific City-State with 3 Envoys.
** +2 Culture for each Cultural City-State with 3 Envoys.
** +4 Gold for each Trade City-State with 3 Envoys.
** +2 Faith for each Religious City-State with 3 Envoys.
