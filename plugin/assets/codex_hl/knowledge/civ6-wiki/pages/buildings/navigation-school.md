## Summary

Sagres school
The Navigation School is a unique building of the Portuguese civilization in Civilization VI. It is built in the Campus district and replaces the University.
*Effects:
** +25% Production toward naval units in this city
**+1 Science for every two Coast or Lake tiles in this city
**+1 Admiral point per turn
** +4 Science (boosted by Rationalism policy card)
**+1 Housing
**+1 Citizen Citizen slot
**+1 Scientist point per turn
** +2 Science for each Scientific City-State with 6 Envoys.
***With Ethiopia Pack: +2 Science for each Scientific City-State with 3 Envoys.
**+2 Science with Great Scientist Isaac Newton activated.
