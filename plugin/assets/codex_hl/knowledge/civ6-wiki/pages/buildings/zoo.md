## Summary

The Zoo is an advanced entertainment building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Entertainment Complex district and requires an Arena (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +1 Amenity from Entertainment to all City Centers within 6 tiles of this Zoo's Entertainment Complex
** +1 Science to each Rainforest and Marsh tile in this city
** +1 Amenity and +3 tiles radius with Great Engineer Joseph Paxton activated.
