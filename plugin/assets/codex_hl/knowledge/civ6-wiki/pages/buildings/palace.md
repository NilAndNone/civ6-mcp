## Summary

The Palace is a special building that serves as your seat of government in Civilization VI. It is built automatically in the City Center of a civilization's current Capital.
* Effects:
** +2 Production
** +1 Culture
** +2 Science
** +5 Gold
** +1 Housing
** +1 Amenity ( +2 Amenities) from Entertainment
** +1 slot for a Great Work (any type)
** +3 Strength Combat Strength for the city ("Palace Guard")
** +1 Loyalty per turn from each Citizen Citizen
