## Summary

A Reef is a terrain feature in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It is found exclusively on Coast tiles.
* Yields:
** +1 Food, +1 Production
* Moves needed:
** 1
* Defense provided:
** +3
* Additional traits:
** Blocks placement of districts and wonders
** Cannot be improved, unless there is a resource on it
** +1 Science with an Aquarium
** +2 Science adjacency bonus for Campuses
