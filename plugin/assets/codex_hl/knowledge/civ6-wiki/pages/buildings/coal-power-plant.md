## Summary

The Coal Power Plant is an advanced production building in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It is the first power-producing building in the game; it is built in the Industrial Zone district and requires a Factory (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** Grants bonus Production equal to the district's current adjacency bonus.
** Converts Coal into Power for this city and other cities whose City Centers are within 6 tiles. (Conversion rate: 1 Coal → 4 Power)
** +1 Citizen Citizen slot.
** +1 Production additionally per Specialist in this district
** +1 Engineer point per turn.
** +2 Culture and +4 Gold with Third Alternative policy card.
** With Ethiopia Pack: +3 Production toward wonders, buildings, and districts for each Industrial City-State with 6 Envoys.
** Heavy CO2 emissions into the atmosphere.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built if Oil Power Plant or Nuclear Power Plant has already been built in this district
