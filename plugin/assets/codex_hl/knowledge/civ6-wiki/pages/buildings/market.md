## Summary

The Market is a basic economic building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Commercial Hub district (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +3 Gold (boosted by Free Market policy card)
** +1 Citizen Icon Citizen slot
** +1 Merchant point per turn.
** +4 Gold for each Trade City-State with 3 Envoys.
*** +2 Gold for each Trade City-State with 3 Envoys.
*** With Ethiopia Pack: +2 Gold for each Trade City-State with 1 Envoy.
** +1 Trade Route capacity if this city doesn't already have a Lighthouse.
