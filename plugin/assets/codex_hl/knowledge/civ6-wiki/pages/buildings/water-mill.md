## Summary

The Water Mill is an early infrastructure building in Civilization VI. It is built in the City Center.
* Effects:
** +1 Food
** +1 Production
** +1 Food from local bonus resources improved by Farms.
* Restrictions:
** City must be adjacent to a River.
