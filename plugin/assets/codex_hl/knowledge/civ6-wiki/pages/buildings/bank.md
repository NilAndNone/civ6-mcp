## Summary

The Bank is an advanced economic building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Commercial Hub district and requires a Market (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +5 Gold (boosted by Free Market policy card)
** +1 Citizen Icon Citizen slot
** +1 Merchant point per turn
** +2 slots for Great Works of any type with Great Merchant Giovanni de' Medici activated.
** +4 Gold for each Trade City-State with 6 Envoys.
*** +2 Gold for each Trade City-State with 6 Envoys.
*** With Ethiopia Pack: +4 Gold for each Trade City-State with 3 Envoys.
