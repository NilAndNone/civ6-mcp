## Summary

The Lighthouse is a basic maritime building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Harbor district (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +1 Food
** +1 Food in Coast and Lake tiles controlled by the city
** +1 Gold
**+1 Housing
**+1 Citizen Citizen slot
** +1 Admiral point per turn.
**+25% combat XP for all naval units trained in this city.
** +1 Trade Route capacity if this city doesn't already have a Market.
** +2 additional Housing if City Center is adjacent to Coast.
** +2 Gold for each Trade City-State with 3 Envoys.
***With Ethiopia Pack: +2 Gold for each Trade City-State with 1 Envoy.
