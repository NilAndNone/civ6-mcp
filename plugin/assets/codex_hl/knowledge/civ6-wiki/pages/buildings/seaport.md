## Summary

The Seaport is an advanced maritime building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Harbor district and requires a Shipyard (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +25% Faster Fleet and Armada training.
** +25% combat experience for all naval units trained in this city.
** +2 Gold to all Coast or Lake tiles owned by this city.
** +2 Food
** +2 Gold
** +1 Housing
** +1 Citizen Citizen slot
** +1 Food additionally per Specialist in this district.
** +1 Admiral point per turn.
** +1 Science ( +2 Science) with Military Research policy card
** +15% Production towards Space Race projects with Integrated Space Cell policy card
** With Ethiopia Pack: +6 Gold for each Trade City-State with 6 Envoys.
In the Monopolies and Corporations game mode, the Seaport can store 3 Product Icon Corporation products.
