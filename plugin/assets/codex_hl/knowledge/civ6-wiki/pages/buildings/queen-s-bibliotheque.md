## Summary

National Library of Sweden
The Queen's Bibliotheque is a unique building of the Swedish civilization (when led by Kristina) in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It is built in the Government Plaza district and requires a Tier 2 government (Merchant Republic, Monarchy, or Theocracy) and a Tier 1 government building (Ancestral Hall, Audience Chamber, or Warlord's Throne).
* Effects:
** +2 Writer points per turn.
** +2 Artist points per turn.
** +2 Musician points per turn.
** +2 Writing Great Work of Writing slots.
** +2 SculpturePortraitLandscapeReligious Art Great Work of Art slots.
** +2 Music Great Work of Music slots.
** Awards +1 Governor Icon Governor Title.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built if Foreign Ministry, Grand Master's Chapel, or Intelligence Agency has already been built in this district.
