## Summary

The Synagogue is an advanced religious building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Holy Site district and requires a Temple (or one of its replacements). It may also be purchased with Faith.
* Effects:
** +5 Faith (boosted by Simultaneum policy card)
** +1 Citizen Citizen slot
** +1 Faith additionally per Specialist in this district
** With Ethiopia Pack: +3 Faith for each Religious City-State with 6 Envoys
* Restrictions:
** Requires the Synagogue Worship Belief to be constructed.
