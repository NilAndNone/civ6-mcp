## Summary

The Chancery is an advanced diplomatic building in Civilization VI. It is built in the Diplomatic Quarter district and requires a Consulate (or one of its replacements).
* Effects:
** +3 Influence Points per turn.
** When this civilization captures or kills an enemy Spy, receive 50 Science for every level of the enemy Spy.
** +3 Production toward units for each Militaristic City-State with 6 Envoys.
** +3 Production toward wonders, buildings, and districts for each Industrial City-State with 6 Envoys.
** +3 Science for each Scientific City-State with 6 Envoys.
** +3 Culture for each Cultural City-State with 6 Envoys.
** +6 Gold for each Trade City-State with 6 Envoys.
** +3 Faith for each Religious City-State with 6 Envoys.
