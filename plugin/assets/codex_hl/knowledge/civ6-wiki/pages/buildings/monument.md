## Summary

The Monument is a basic cultural building in Civilization VI. It is built in the City Center and does not require any technological research.

## Key Facts

- Era: Ancient
- Cost: 60
- Unique district: City Center
- effect: +2 Culture
 +1 Loyalty and +1 Culture. +1 additionnal Culture if city is at maximum Loyalty.
- uniquevariants: Old God Obelisk,Voidsingers
