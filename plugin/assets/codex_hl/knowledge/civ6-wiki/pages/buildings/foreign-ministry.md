## Summary

The Foreign Ministry is a Tier 2 government building in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It is built in the Government Plaza district and requires a Tier 2 government (Merchant Republic, Monarchy, or Theocracy) and a Tier 1 government building (Ancestral Hall, Audience Chamber, or Warlord's Throne).
* Effects:
** Levying City-States costs half Gold.
** City-State units gain +4 Strength Combat Strength if you are the City-State's Suzerain (including levied units).
** Awards +1 Governor Icon Governor Title.
** +3 Diplomatic Favor per turn.
* Restrictions:
** Cannot be built if Grand Master's Chapel, Intelligence Agency, or Queen's Bibliotheque has already been built in this district.
