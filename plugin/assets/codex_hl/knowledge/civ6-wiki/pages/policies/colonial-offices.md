## Summary

Colonial Offices is an Economic Policy Card in Civilization VI. In Gathering Storm, it becomes a Diplomatic Policy Card.

## Key Facts

- Era: Renaissance
- enabled with: Exploration
- type: Economic
- effect: +15% faster growth for cities not on your original Capital's continent.
 +15% faster growth and 3 Loyalty per turn for cities not on your original Capital's continent.
