## Summary

Simultaneum
Simultaneum is an Economic Policy Card in Civilization VI.

## Key Facts

- Era: Renaissance
- enabled with: Reformed Church
- type: Economic
- effect: +100% Faith from Holy Site district buildings.
 Extra Faith from buildings in Holy Sites: +50% if city Citizen population is 10 or higher, +50% if district has at least +3 adjacency bonus.
 Extra Faith from buildings in Holy Sites: +50% if city Citizen population is 15 or higher, +50% if district has at least +4 adjacency bonus.
