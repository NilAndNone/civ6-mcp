## Summary

Resource management
Resource Management is an Economic Policy Card in Civilization VI. In Gathering Storm, it becomes a Military Policy Card.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- enabled with: Conservation
- type: Economic
- effect: 1 copy of a Strategic resource allows you to produce and purchase units requiring it in any city.
 All improved Aluminum and Oil resources yield 1 additional resource per turn.
