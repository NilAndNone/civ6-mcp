## Summary

Laissez-faire
Laissez-Faire is a Wildcard Policy Card in Civilization VI.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- type: Wildcard
- enabled with: Capitalism
- effect: +4 Merchant points per turn.
 +2 Merchant points for every Bank and +4 Merchant points for every Stock Exchange. +2 Admiral points for every Shipyard and +4 Admiral points for every Seaport.
- notes: Requires available Great Merchant to recruit.
