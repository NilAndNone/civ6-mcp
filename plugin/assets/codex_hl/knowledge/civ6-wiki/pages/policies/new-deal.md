## Summary

New Deal
New Deal is an Economic Policy Card in Civilization VI. In Gathering Storm, it is only available after adopting Democracy as a government.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- type: Economic
- enabled with: Suffrage
- government: Democracy
- effect: +4 Housing, +2 Amenities, -8 Gold to all cities with at least 3 specialty districts.
