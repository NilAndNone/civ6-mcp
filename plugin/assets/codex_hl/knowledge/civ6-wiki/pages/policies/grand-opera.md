## Summary

Grand opera
Grand Opera is an Economic Policy Card in Civilization VI.

## Key Facts

- Era: Industrial
- enabled with: Opera and Ballet
- type: Economic
- effect: +100% Culture from Theater Square district buildings.
 Extra Culture from buildings in Theater Squares: +50% if city Citizen population is 10 or higher, +50% if district has at least +3 adjacency bonus.
 Extra Culture from buildings in Theater Squares: +50% if city Citizen population is 15 or higher, +50% if district has at least +4 adjacency bonus.
