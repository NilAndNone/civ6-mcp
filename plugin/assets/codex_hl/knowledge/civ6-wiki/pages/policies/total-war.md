## Summary

Total war
Total War is a Military Policy Card in Civilization VI. In the February 2019 Update, it was updated to provide bonus yields from coastal raids and plundering Trade Routes.

## Key Facts

- Era: Industrial
- enabled with: Scorched Earth
- type: Military
- effect: Yields gained from pillaging and coastal raids are +100% ( +50%). +50% rewards for plundering Trade Routes.
