## Summary

Raid (military)
Raid is a Military Policy Card in Civilization VI.
In the February 7, 2018 Update, Raid gained the effect of doubling yields from pillaging Districts when Sack was removed from the game. Prior to that, it only doubled yields from pillaging tile improvements.

## Key Facts

- Era: Classical
- enabled with: Military Training
- obsolete with: Scorched Earth
- type: Military
- effect: Yields gained from pillaging are doubled for pillaging improvements and Districts.
 Yields gained from pillaging and coastal raids are +50%.
