Policy cards are a new gameplay mechanic introduced in Civilization VI, and are part of the new government system of the game. They represent the new incarnation of social policies, the actual effects of your political agenda, and in many cases are referred to as "policies," both to shorten the name and to keep the tradition of previous games.
