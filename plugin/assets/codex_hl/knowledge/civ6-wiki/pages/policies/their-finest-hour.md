## Summary

This was their finest hour
Their Finest Hour is a Military Policy Card in Civilization VI. In Gathering Storm, it becomes a Wildcard Policy Card and is only available after adopting Democracy as a government.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- enabled with: Ideology
- obsolete with: Globalization
- government: Democracy
- type: Military
- effect: +50% Production toward Modern and Atomic air units.
 All units +5 Strength Combat Strength when fighting in or adjacent to their home territory.
