## Summary

Arsenal of Democracy
Arsenal of Democracy is a Diplomatic Policy Card in Civilization VI.
In Gathering Storm, Arsenal of Democracy is removed from the game and its effects are transferred to Democracy, the Tier 3 government.

## Key Facts

- removed: Gathering Storm
- Era: Modern
- enabled with: Suffrage
- type: Diplomatic
- effect: Your Trade Routes to an Ally's or Suzerian's city provide +2 Food and +2 Production for both cities.
 Your Trade Routes to an Ally's or Suzerian's city provide +4 Food and +4 Production for both cities. Alliance Points with each ally grow 25% faster.
