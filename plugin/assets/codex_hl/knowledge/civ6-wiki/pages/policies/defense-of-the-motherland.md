## Summary

Homeland defense
 is a Military Policy Card in Civilization VI. In Gathering Storm, it becomes a Wildcard Policy Card and is only available after adopting Communism as a government.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- enabled with: Class Struggle
- government: Communism
- type: Military
- effect: No war weariness from combat in your territory.
 No war weariness from combat in your territory. +100% Production for Modern, Atomic, and Information era support units.
