## Summary

Martial law
Martial Law is a Military Policy Card in Civilization VI. In Gathering Storm, it becomes a Wildcard Policy Card and is only available after adopting Fascism as a government.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- enabled with: Totalitarianism
- government: Fascism
- type: Military
- effect: Accumulate 25% less war weariness than usual.
