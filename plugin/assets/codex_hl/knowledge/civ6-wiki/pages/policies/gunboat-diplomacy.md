## Summary

Gunboat diplomacy
Gunboat Diplomacy is a Diplomatic Policy Card in Civilization VI. In Gathering Storm, it is unlocked with Ideology.

## Key Facts

- Era: Modern
- type: Diplomatic
- enabled with: Totalitarianism
- effect: Open Borders with all city-states, and +4 Influence Points per turn toward earning Envoys.
