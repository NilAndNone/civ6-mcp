## Summary

Science Foundations is a Wildcard Policy Card in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It replaced Nobel Prize.

## Key Facts

- Introduced in: Gathering Storm
- Era: Modern
- enabled with: Nuclear Program
- type: Wildcard
- effect: +2 Scientist points for every University and +4 Scientist points for every Research Lab.
+2 Engineer points for every Factory and +4 Engineer points for every Power Plant.
- notes: Requires available Great Scientist to recruit.
