## Summary

Sun Tzu
Sun Tzu is a Classical Era Great General in Civilization VI. His unit provides +5 Strength Combat Strength and +1 Movement to Classical and Medieval era land units within 2 tiles.
Sun Tzu can be retired at a district or wonder with an available Great Work slot to create The Art of War, a Writing Icon Great Work of Writing that provides +4 Culture and +4 Tourism.

## Key Facts

- type: Great General
- Era: Classical
- movement: 4
- unit bonuses: +5 Strength Combat Strength and +1 Movement to Classical and Medieval era land units within 2 tiles.
- retire effect: Creates the Art of War Great Work of Writing (+4 Culture, +4 Tourism).
- notes: Boosts combat strength and mobility of nearby land units. Can "Retire" to expend it once no longer useful.
