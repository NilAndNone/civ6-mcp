## Summary

Brazilian Carnival
Carnival is a unique project of the Brazilian civilization in Civilization VI. It becomes available after building a Street Carnival or Copacabana district.
Let the samba reign supreme! This unique project unleashes wild festivities in the city which increase the city's Amenities by 1 while underway. When completed, it yields Engineer, Merchant, Writer, Artist, and Musician points each equal to approximately 11% of the Production invested in it (on Standard speed).
