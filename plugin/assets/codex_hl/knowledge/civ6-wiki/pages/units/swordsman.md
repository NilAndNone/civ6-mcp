## Summary

The Swordsman is a Classical Era melee unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Warrior (or its replacements, except the Gaesatae) or the Sabum Kibittum and requires Iron.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Swordsman requires 20 Iron to train.
* Attributes:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
