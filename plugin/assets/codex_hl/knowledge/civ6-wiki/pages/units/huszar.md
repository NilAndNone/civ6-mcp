## Summary

Hussar
The Huszár is a unique light cavalry unit of the Hungarian civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It replaces the Cavalry and requires 10 Horses to train.
* Common abilities:
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
* Special abilities:
**+3 Strength Combat Strength for every active Alliance.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (65 vs. 62).
** Higher Production cost (335 vs. 330).
