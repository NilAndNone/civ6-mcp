## Summary

The Cavalry is an Industrial Era light cavalry unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Horseman (or its replacements, except for the Hetairoi) or the Malón Raider and requires Horses.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Cavalry primarily upgrades from the Courser (or its replacements, except for the Black Army) or the Malón Raider and requires 20 Horses to train.

** Ignores enemy zone of control.
