## Summary

Hoplite
The Hoplite is a unique anti-cavalry unit of the Greek civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Spearman.
* Common abilities:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength vs. light, heavy, and ranged cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength when adjacent to at least one other Hoplite.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (28 vs. 25).
