## Summary

Impi
The Impi is a unique anti-cavalry unit of the Zulu civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It replaces the Pikeman.
* Common traits:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength vs. light, heavy, and ranged cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** Gains 25% more experience.
* Special traits:
** Lower Production cost (125 vs. 200).
** Lower Gold maintenance cost (1 vs. 3).
** Increased flanking bonus (4 vs. 2).
