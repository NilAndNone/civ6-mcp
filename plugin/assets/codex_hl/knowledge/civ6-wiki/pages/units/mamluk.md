## Summary

Mamluk
The Mamluk is a unique heavy cavalry unit of the Arabian civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Knight.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Mamluk's Production cost is increased from 180 to 220, its Gold purchasing cost is increased from 720 to 880, its Gold maintenance cost is increased from 3 to 4, and it requires 10 Iron to train.
* Common abilities:
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
* Special abilities:
** Heals at the end of every turn, even if it moves or attacks.
