## Summary

Red coat (military uniform)
The Redcoat is a unique melee unit of the English civilization (when led by Victoria (Age of Empire)) in Civilization VI. It replaces the Line Infantry.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Redcoat requires 10 Niter to train.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** No Moves cost to disembark.
** +10 Strength Combat Strength when fighting on a continent other than the English Capital's.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (70 vs. 65).
