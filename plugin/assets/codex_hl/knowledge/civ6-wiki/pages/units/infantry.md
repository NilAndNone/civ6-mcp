## Summary

The Infantry is a Modern Era melee unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Line Infantry (or its replacements).
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Infantry requires 1 Oil to train and 1 Oil per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
