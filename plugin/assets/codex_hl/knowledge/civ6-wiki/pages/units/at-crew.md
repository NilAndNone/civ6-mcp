## Summary

The AT Crew (short for "Anti-Tank Crew") is a Modern Era anti-cavalry unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Pikeman (or its replacements).
In the Rise and Fall expansion, the AT Crew primarily upgrades from the Pike and Shot.
* Attributes:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength vs. light, heavy, and ranged cavalry units.
