## Summary

U-boat
The U-Boat is a unique naval raider unit of the German civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Submarine.
* Common abilities:
** Invisible except when within Sight range of Destroyers, other naval raiders, or when there is a unit in an adjacent tile.
** Reveals stealth units within Sight range.
** Can perform Coastal Raids.
** Has a ranged attack with Range 2.
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
** Does not exert zone of control.
* Special abilities:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength when fighting on Ocean tiles.
* Special traits:
** Higher Sight (3 vs. 2).
** Lower Production cost (430 vs. 480).
** No strategic resource requirement.
