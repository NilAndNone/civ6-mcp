## Summary

The Destroyer is an Atomic Era naval melee unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Ironclad (or its replacements).
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Destroyer requires 1 Oil to train and 1 Oil per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** Has Sight of 3.
** Reveals stealth units within Sight range.
** Can counter air units.
