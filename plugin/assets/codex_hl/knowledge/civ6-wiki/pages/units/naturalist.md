## Summary

The Naturalist is a Modern Era civilian unit in Civilization VI. It can only be purchased with Faith in any city.
* Attributes:
** Has Sight of 3.
* Abilities:
** Can designate a site for a National Park. This is a one-time use Ability, after which the Naturalist is consumed.
