## Summary

Khevsurians
The Khevsur is a unique melee unit of the Georgian civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It replaces the Man-At-Arms and is unlocked with Military Tactics.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Khevsur requires 10 Iron to train.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
* Special abilites:
** No Moves penalty in Hills.
** +7 Strength Combat Strength when fighting in Hills.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (48 vs. 45).
