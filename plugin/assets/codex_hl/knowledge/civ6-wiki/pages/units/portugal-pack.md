## Summary

The Portugal Pack is DLC for Civilization VI that was released on March 25th, 2021 as the sixth and final part of New Frontier Pass. The pack includes:
* The Portuguese civilization led by João III
* The Zombie Defense game mode
* Two new Wonders: Torre de Belém and Etemenanki
* The Wetlands map
The Portugal Pack DLC was released simultaneously with the Civilization VI March 2021 Update. The DLC is not required to get the update.
