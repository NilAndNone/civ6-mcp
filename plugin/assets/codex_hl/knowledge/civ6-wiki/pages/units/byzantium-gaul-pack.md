## Summary

The Byzantium & Gaul Pack is DLC for Civilization VI that was released on September 24, 2020, as the third part of New Frontier Pass. The pack includes:
* The Byzantine civilization led by Basil II and the Gallic civilization led by Ambiorix.
* The Dramatic Ages game mode.
* Two new Wonders: the Biosphère and the Statue of Zeus.
* The Highlands map.
The Byzantium & Gaul Pack DLC was released simultaneously with the Civilization VI September 2020 Update. The DLC is not required to get the update.
