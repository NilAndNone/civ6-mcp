## Summary

Royal Canadian Mounted Police
The Mountie is a unique light cavalry unit of the Canadian civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm.
* Attributes:
** Has Sight of 4.
** +5 Strength Combat Strength when within 2 tiles of a National Park. +5 additional Strength Combat Strength if the park is Canadian.
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
* Abilities:
** Has 2 Build Charges Icon build charges.
** Can establish a National Park. (Consumes a Build Charges Icon build charge.)
