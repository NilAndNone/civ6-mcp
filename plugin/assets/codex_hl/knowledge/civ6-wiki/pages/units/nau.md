## Summary

Nau (ship)
The Nau is a unique naval melee unit of the Portuguese civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Caravel.
* Common abilities:
** Has Sight of 3.
* Special abilities:
** Receives one free Promotion when first trained, purchased, or upgraded from an earlier unit.
** Has 2 Build Charges Icon build charges.
** Can construct a Feitoria (Consumes a Build Charges Icon build charge).
* Special traits:
**Lower Gold maintenance cost (2 vs. 4).
