## Summary

Bireme
The Bireme is a unique naval melee unit of the Phoenician civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It replaces the Galley.
* Common abilities:
** Cannot enter Ocean tiles until Cartography has been researched.
* Special abilities:
** Prevents friendly Traders within 4 tiles from being plundered as long as the Traders are on a water tile.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (35 vs. 30).
** Higher Moves Icon​​ Movement (4 vs. 3).
