## Summary

War elephant
The Varu is a unique heavy cavalry unit of the Indian civilization in Civilization VI.
* Attributes:
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
** Reduces the Strength Combat Strength of adjacent enemy units by 5 ( non-cumulatively; cumulatively under other rulesets).
