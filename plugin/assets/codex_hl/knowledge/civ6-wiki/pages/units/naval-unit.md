## Summary

A naval unit is (with the exception of the Great Admiral) a military unit in Civilization VI and its expansions which has water as its native element: it can never move onto a land tile unless that tile is a City Center, a Canal, or submerged due to rising sea levels. Unlike in Civilization V, no naval units (including Submarines) can move through Ice tiles.
