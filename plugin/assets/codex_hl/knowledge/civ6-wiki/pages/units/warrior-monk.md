## Summary

Warrior monk
The Warrior Monk is a special unit in Civilization VI, introduced in the Fall 2017 Update. It can only be purchased with Faith in a city that has a majority religion with the Warrior Monks Follower Belief and a Holy Site with a Temple (or one of its replacements).
* Attributes:
** Follows a unique promotion table.
** +10 Strength Icon Combat Strength if Outcome A of the World Religion Resolution is passed.
