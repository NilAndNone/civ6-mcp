## Summary

The Scout is an Ancient Era recon unit in Civilization VI. It is available without any technological research.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Scout upgrades to the Skirmisher.
* Attributes:
** Has 3 Moves.
** Costs no Gold to maintain.
** Gains XP when activating Tribal Villages (+5 XP) and discovering natural wonders (+10 XP), besides the normal gains from combat.
