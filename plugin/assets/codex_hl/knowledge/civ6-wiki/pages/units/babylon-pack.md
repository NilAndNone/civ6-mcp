## Summary

The Babylon Pack is DLC for Civilization VI that was released on November 19, 2020, as the fourth part of New Frontier Pass. The pack includes:
*The Babylonian civilization led by Hammurabi
*The Heroes & Legends game mode
*New City-states: Ayutthaya, Chinguetti, Johannesburg, Nalanda, Samarkand and Wolin.
*New Great People:
**Great Admirals: Hanno the Navigator, Himerios, Matthew Perry
**Great Artists: Hasegawa Tōhaku, Kamāl ud-Dīn Behzād, Wassily Kandinsky
**Great Engineers: Imhotep, Kenzo Tange, Shah Jahān
**Great Generals: Dandara, Trưng Trắc, Tupac Amaru
**Great Merchants: Ibn Fadlan, Stamford Raffles, Zhou Daguan
**Great Musicians: Dimitrie Cantemir, Scott Joplin
**Great Scientists: Ibn Khaldun, Margaret Mead, Zhang Heng
**Great Writers: Beatrix Potter, Gabriela Mistral, Rumi, Valmiki
The Babylon Pack DLC was released simultaneously with the Civilization VI November 2020 Update. The DLC is not required to get the update.
