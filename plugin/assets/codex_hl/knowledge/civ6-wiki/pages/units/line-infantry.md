## Summary

The Line Infantry is an Industrial Era melee unit in Civilization VI, introduced in the April 2021 Update. It upgrades from the Musketman (or its replacements) and requires Niter.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Line Infantry requires 20 Niter to train.
* Attributes:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
