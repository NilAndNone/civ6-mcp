## Summary

Barbary pirates
The Barbary Corsair is a unique naval raider unit of the Ottoman civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It replaces the Privateer and is unlocked with Medieval Faires.
* Common abilities:
** Invisible except when within Sight range of Destroyers, other naval raiders, or when there is a unit in an adjacent tile.
** Reveals stealth units within Sight range.
** Can perform Coastal Raids.
** Has a ranged attack with Range 2.
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
* Special abilities:
** Uses no Moves to perform coastal raids.
* Special traits:
** Lower Production cost (240 vs. 280).
** Lower Gold maintenance cost (3 vs. 4).
