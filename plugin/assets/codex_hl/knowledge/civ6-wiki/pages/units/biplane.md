## Summary

The Biplane is a Modern Era air fighter unit in Civilization VI.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Biplane requires 1 Oil to train and 1 Oil per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** Has Sight of 4.
** Has Range of 4.
** -17 Ranged Strength Ranged Strength against District defenses.
* Abilities:
** May perform Patrols to Intercept incoming enemy aircraft.
