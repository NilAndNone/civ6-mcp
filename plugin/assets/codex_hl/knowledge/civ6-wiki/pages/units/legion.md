## Summary

Roman legion
The Legion is a unique melee unit of the Roman civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Swordsman.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Legion requires 10 Iron to train.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** Has 1 Build Charges Icon build charge.
** Can construct a Roman Fort. (Consumes build charge.)
** Can clear terrain. (Consumes build charge.)
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (40 vs. 35).
** Higher Production cost (110 vs. 90).
