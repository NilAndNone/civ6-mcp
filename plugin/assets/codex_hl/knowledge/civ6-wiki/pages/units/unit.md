## Summary

A unit is an agent of a civilization or NPC faction (i.e., a city-state, a Free City, or the Barbarians) that can move around the map and be given commands.
Civilization VI follows most of the established traditions of the series regarding units, while also introducing some new mechanics. Unit models are a little different than they were in previous games, featuring larger models with more exaggerated features, and generally fewer (but more detailed) character models per unit. Unit animations are also more elaborate than in previous entries - melee units, for example, have special finishing animations that play when they destroy enemy units.
