## Summary

The Vietnam & Kublai Khan Pack is DLC for Civilization VI that was released on January 28, 2021, as the fifth part of New Frontier Pass. The pack includes:
* The Vietnamese civilization led by Bà Triệu
* Kublai Khan, an alternate leader for the Chinese and Mongolian civilizations
* The Monopolies and Corporations game mode.
* The Preserve district.
* The Grove and Sanctuary buildings.
 Requires the Rise and Fall expansion to play.;
The Vietnam & Kublai Khan Pack DLC was released simultaneously with the Civilization VI January 2021 Update. The DLC is not required to get the update.
