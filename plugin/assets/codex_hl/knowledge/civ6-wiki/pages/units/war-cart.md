## Summary

Chariot
The War-Cart is a unique heavy cavalry unit of the Sumerian civilization in Civilization VI. It is available without any technological research.
* Attributes:
** Strongest of the starting units in the game.
** No combat penalties against anti-cavalry units.
** +1 Moves when starting its turn on flat terrain with no Woods or Rainforests.
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
