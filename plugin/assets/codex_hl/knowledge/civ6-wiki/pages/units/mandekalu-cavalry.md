## Summary

Military history of the Mali Empire
The Mandekalu Cavalry is a unique heavy cavalry unit of the Malian civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It replaces the Knight and requires 10 Iron to train.
* Common abilities:
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
* Special abilities:
** Prevents friendly Traders within 4 tiles from being plundered as long as they are on a land tile.
** Provides Gold equal to 100% of the base Strength Combat Strength of each defeated enemy unit.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (55 vs. 50).
