## Summary

Sling (weapon)
The Warak'aq is a unique recon unit of the Incan civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It replaces the Skirmisher.
* Common abilities:
** Has a ranged attack with Range 1.
** -17 Ranged Strength Ranged Strength against District defenses and naval units.
** Gains XP when activating Tribal Villages (+5 XP) and discovering natural wonders (+10 XP), besides the normal gains from combat.
* Special abilities:
** Can make 1 additional attack per turn if Moves allows.
* Special traits:
** Higher Production cost (165 vs. 150).
** Higher Ranged Strength Ranged Strength (40 vs. 30).
