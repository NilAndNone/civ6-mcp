## Summary

Imperial Guard (Napoleon I)
The Garde Impériale is a unique melee unit of the French civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Line Infantry.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Garde Impériale requires 10 Niter to train.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** Earns General points from kills.
** +10 Strength Combat Strength on the original Capital's home continent.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (70 vs. 65).
