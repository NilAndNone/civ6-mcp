## Summary

Hwacha
The Hwacha is a unique ranged unit of the Korean civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It replaces the Field Cannon and is unlocked with Gunpowder.
* Common abilities:
** -17 Ranged Strength Ranged Strength against District defenses and naval units.
* Special abilities:
** Cannot attack after moving unless its maximum Moves is 3 or more.
* Special traits:
** Lower Production cost (250 vs. 330).
** Lower Strength Combat Strength (45 vs. 50).
** Lower Gold maintenance cost (3 vs. 5).
