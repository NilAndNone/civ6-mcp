## Summary

Dromon
The Dromon is a unique naval ranged unit of the Byzantine civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Quadrireme.
* Common abilities:
** Cannot enter Ocean tiles until Cartography has been researched.
* Special abilities:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength against land and naval units.
* Special traits:
** Has a ranged attack with Range 2.
