## Summary

Maryannu
The Maryannu Chariot Archer is a unique ranged cavalry unit of the Egyptian civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Heavy Chariot.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Maryannu Chariot Archer becomes a standalone unique unit and no longer replaces the Heavy Chariot.
