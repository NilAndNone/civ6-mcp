## Summary

The Aircraft Carrier is an Atomic Era naval carrier unit in Civilization VI. It requires Oil.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Aircraft Carrier does not require any strategic resources.
* Attributes:
** Can melee attack and take cities.
** Can carry up to two air units (more with Promotions).
** Cannot combine to form Fleets or Armadas after being trained.
