## Summary

The Observation Balloon is a Modern Era support unit in Civilization VI.
In the Rise and Fall expansion, the Observation Balloon upgrades to the Drone.
* Attributes:
** Has Sight of 3.
** Sight is unobscured by terrain features.
** Adds +1 Range to adjacent bombard class units.
