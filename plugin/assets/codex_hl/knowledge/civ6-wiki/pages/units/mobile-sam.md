## Summary

The Mobile SAM (short for "Mobile Surface-to-Air Missile") is an Information Era support unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Anti-Air Gun.
* Attributes:
** Protects all units within 1 tile from nuclear strikes and attacks by air units.
