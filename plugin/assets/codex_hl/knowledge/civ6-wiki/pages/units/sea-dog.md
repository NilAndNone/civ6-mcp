## Summary

Elizabethan Sea Dogs
The Sea Dog is a unique naval raider unit of the English civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Privateer.
* Common abilities:
** Invisible except when within Sight range of Destroyers, other naval raiders, or when there is a unit in an adjacent tile.
** Reveals stealth units within Sight range.
** Can perform Coastal Raids.
** Has a ranged attack with Range 2.
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
* Special abilities:
** Higher Ranged Strength Ranged Strength (55 vs. 50).
** Can capture defeated enemy naval units. (Does not work for Barbarian or city-state ships.)
