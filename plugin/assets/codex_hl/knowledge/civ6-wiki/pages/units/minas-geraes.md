## Summary

Minas Geraes-class battleship
The Minas Geraes is a unique naval ranged unit of the Brazilian civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Battleship and is unlocked with Nationalism.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Minas Geraes requires 1 Coal to train and 1 Coal per turn to maintain.
* Common abilities:
** Has a ranged attack with Range 3.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (70 vs. 60).
** Higher Ranged Strength Ranged Strength (80 vs. 70).
** Higher Antiair Strength Anti-Air strength (95 vs. 90).
