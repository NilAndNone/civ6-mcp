## Summary

The Ironclad is an Industrial Era naval melee unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Caravel (or its replacements) and requires Coal.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Ironclad requires 1 Coal to train and 1 Coal per turn to maintain.
