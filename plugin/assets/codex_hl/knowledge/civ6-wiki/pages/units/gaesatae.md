## Summary

Gaesatae
The Gaesatae is a unique melee unit of the Gallic civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Warrior.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
** Costs no Gold to maintain.
* Special abilities:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength when fighting units with a higher base Strength Combat Strength.
** +5 Strength Combat Strength when attacking and defending against District defenses.
* Special traits:
** Higher Production cost (60 vs. 40).
