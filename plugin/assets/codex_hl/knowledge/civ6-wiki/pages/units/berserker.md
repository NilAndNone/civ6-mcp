## Summary

Berserker
The is a unique melee unit of the Norwegian civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Man-At-Arms and is unlocked with Military Tactics.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Berserker requires 10 Iron to train.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** +2 Moves when starting its turn in enemy territory.
** +10 Strength Combat Strength when attacking.
** -5 Strength Combat Strength when defending against melee attacks.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (48 vs. 45).
