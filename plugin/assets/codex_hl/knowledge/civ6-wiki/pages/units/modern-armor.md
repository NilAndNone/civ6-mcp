## Summary

The Modern Armor is an Information Era heavy cavalry unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Tank (or its replacements) and requires Uranium.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Modern Armor requires 1 Oil to train and 1 Oil per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** +1 Moves when starting its turn on flat terrain with no Woods or Rainforests.
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
