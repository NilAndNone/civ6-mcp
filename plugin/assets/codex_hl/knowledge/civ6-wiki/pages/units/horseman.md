## Summary

The Horseman is a Classical Era light cavalry unit in Civilization VI. It requires Horses.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Horseman upgrades to the Courser and requires 20 Horses to train.
* Attributes:
** First unit with a Moves of 4.
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
