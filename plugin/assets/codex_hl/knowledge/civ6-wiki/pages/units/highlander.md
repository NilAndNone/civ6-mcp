## Summary

42nd Regiment of Foot
The Highlander is a unique recon unit of the Scottish civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It replaces the Ranger.
* Common abilities:
** Has a ranged attack with Range 1.
** -17 Ranged Strength Ranged Strength against District defenses and naval units.
** Gains XP when activating Tribal Villages (+5 XP) and discovering natural wonders (+10 XP), besides the normal gains from combat.
* Special abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength in Hills and Woods.
** Exerts zone of control.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (50 vs. 45).
** Higher Ranged Strength Ranged Strength (65 vs. 60).
