## Summary

The Maya & Gran Colombia Pack is DLC for Civilization VI that was released on May 21, 2020, as the first part of New Frontier Pass. The pack includes:
* The Mayan civilization led by Lady Six Sky and the Gran Colombian civilization led by Simón Bolívar
* The Apocalypse game mode
* New City-states: Vatican City, Taruga, Hunza, Singapore, Caguana, and Lahore
* New Resources: Maize and Honey
* New Natural Wonders: Païtiti, Bermuda Triangle and Fountain of Youth
The Maya & Gran Colombia Pack DLC was released simultaneously with the Civilization VI May 2020 Update. The DLC is not required to get the update.
