## Summary

Caroleans
The Carolean is a unique anti-cavalry unit of the Swedish civilization in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It replaces the Pike and Shot.
* Common abilities:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength vs. light, heavy, and ranged cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** +3 Strength Combat Strength per point of unused Moves.
* Special traits:
** Higher Moves (3 vs. 2).
** Lower Gold maintenance cost (3 vs. 4).
