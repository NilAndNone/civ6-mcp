## Summary

The Artillery is a Modern Era siege unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Bombard (or its replacements).
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Artillery requires 1 Oil to train and 1 Oil per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** Deals bombard-type damage, effective against District defenses.
** -17 Bombard Strength Bombard Strength against land units.
** Without the Expert Crew Promotion, cannot attack after moving unless its maximum Moves is 3 or more.
