## Summary

Dutch ship De Zeven Provinciën (1665)
The De Zeven Provinciën is a unique naval ranged unit of the Dutch civilization in Civilization VI: Rise and Fall. It replaces the Frigate.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the De Zeven Provinciën requires 10 Niter to train.
* Common abilities:
** Has a ranged attack with Range 2.
* Special abilities:
** +7 Ranged Strength Ranged Strength when attacking and defending against defensible Districts.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (50 vs. 45).
** Higher Ranged Strength Ranged Strength (60 vs. 55).
