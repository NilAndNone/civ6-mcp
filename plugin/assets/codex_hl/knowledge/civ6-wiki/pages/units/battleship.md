## Summary

The Battleship is a Modern Era naval ranged unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Frigate (or its replacements) and requires Coal.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Battleship is unlocked with Refining, and requires 1 Coal to train and 1 Coal per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** Has a ranged attack with Range 3.
** Can counter air units.
