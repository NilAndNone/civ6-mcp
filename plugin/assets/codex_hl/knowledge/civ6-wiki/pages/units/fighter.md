## Summary

The Fighter is an Atomic Era air fighter unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Biplane (or its replacements) and requires Aluminum.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Fighter requires 1 Aluminum to train and 1 Aluminum per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** Has Sight of 4.
** Has Range of 5.
** -17 Ranged Strength Ranged Strength against District defenses.
* Abilities:
** May perform Patrols to Intercept incoming enemy aircraft.
