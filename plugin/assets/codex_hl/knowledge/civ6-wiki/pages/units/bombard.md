## Summary

The Bombard is a Renaissance Era siege unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Trebuchet (or its replacements) and requires Niter.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Bombard requires 20 Niter to train.
* Attributes:
** Deals bombard-type damage, effective against District defenses.
** -17 Bombard Strength Bombard Strength against land units.
** Without the Expert Crew Promotion, cannot attack after moving unless its maximum Moves is 3 or more.
