## Summary

The Missile Cruiser is an Information Era naval ranged unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Battleship (or its replacements).
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Missile Cruiser requires 1 Oil to train and 1 Oil per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** Has a ranged attack with Range 3.
** Can counter air units.
