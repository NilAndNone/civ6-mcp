## Summary

The Settler is an Ancient Era civilian unit in Civilization VI. It is available without any technological research.
* Attributes:
** Has Sight of 3.
* Abilities:
** Found a City on a valid land tile. (Expends the unit and creates a city on its tile.)
** Can only be trained in cities with a Citizen Population of 2 or more.

## Key Facts

- type: Civilian
- Era: Ancient
- production: 80
- gold: 320
- moves: 2
- sight: 3
- notes: May create new cities. Reduces city's citizen Population by 1 when completed. Requires at least 2 citizen Population.; Production cost is progressive.
