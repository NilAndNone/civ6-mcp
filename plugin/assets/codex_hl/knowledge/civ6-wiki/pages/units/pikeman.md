## Summary

The Pikeman is a Medieval Era anti-cavalry unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Spearman (or its replacements).
In the Rise and Fall expansion, the Pikeman can upgrade to a Pike and Shot. In the Gathering Storm expansion, its Production cost is decreased from 200 to 180, its Gold purchasing cost is decreased from 800 to 720, and its Gold maintenance cost is decreased from 3 to 2.
* Attributes:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength vs. light, heavy, and ranged cavalry units.
