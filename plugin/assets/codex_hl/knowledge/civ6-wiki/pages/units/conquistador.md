## Summary

Conquistador
The Conquistador is a unique melee unit of the Spanish civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Musketman.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Conquistador requires 10 Niter to train.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** +10 Strength Combat Strength when within 1 tile of a religious unit.
** Converts cities to Spain's majority religion if it captures a city or is adjacent to the city when it is captured.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (58 vs. 55).
** Higher Production cost (250 vs. 240).
