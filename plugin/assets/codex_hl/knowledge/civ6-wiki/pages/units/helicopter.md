## Summary

The Helicopter is an Atomic Era light cavalry unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Cavalry (or its replacements).
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Helicopter requires 1 Aluminum to train and 1 Aluminum per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** Has Sight of 3.
** All terrain tiles cost 1 Moves (including crossing Rivers).
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
