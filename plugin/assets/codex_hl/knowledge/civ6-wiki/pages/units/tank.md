## Summary

The Tank is a Modern Era heavy cavalry unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Knight (or its replacements), Varu, Winged Hussar, or Rough Rider and requires Oil.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Tank upgrades from the Cuirassier (or its replacements). It requires 1 Oil to train and 1 Oil per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** +1 Moves when starting its turn on flat terrain with no Woods or Rainforests.
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
