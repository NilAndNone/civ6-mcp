## Summary

notes = Performs secret missions in foreign cities and protects your cities from enemy Spies.; Production cost is progressive.
The Spy is a Renaissance Era civilian unit in Civilization VI. It manifests the game's espionage system.
* Attributes:
** Cannot be purchased with Gold.
** Has Sight of 3.
* Abilities:
** Can perform Espionage offensive missions in foreign cities (and city-states, in Rise and Fall).
** Can perform Counter-Espionage defensive missions in your own cities.
