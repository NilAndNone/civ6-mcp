## Summary

Nihang
The Nihang is a unique melee unit of the Lahore city-state. It can be purchased with Faith by a player that is the Suzerain of Lahore.
* Attributes:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
** +15 base Strength Combat Strength every time when Barracks, Armory, and Military Academy are first constructed.
** Follows a unique promotion table.
