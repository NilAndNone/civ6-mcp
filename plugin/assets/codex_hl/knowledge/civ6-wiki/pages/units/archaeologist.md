## Summary

The Archaeologist is an Industrial Era civilian unit in Civilization VI. It can only be trained in a city that has a Theater Square with an Archaeological Museum (with at least one free slot).
* Attributes:
** Has 3 charges (usually; see below), or 6 if trained by the English (in vanilla and Rise and Fall only)
* Abilities:
** Extract Artifact Artifacts from Antiquity Sites and Shipwrecks. (Consumes 1 charge.)
