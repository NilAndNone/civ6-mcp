## Summary

Artists
The is a type of Great Person in Civilization VI dedicated to the creation of Great Work Art Great Works of Art. Each Great Artist may be activated up to three times on a tile containing an empty slot which accepts Great Works of Art (such as a Theater Square district with an Art Museum, a Palace, or the Hermitage), creating up to three Great Works of Art.
