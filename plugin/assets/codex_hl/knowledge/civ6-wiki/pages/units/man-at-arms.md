## Summary

The Man-At-Arms is a Medieval Era melee unit in Civilization VI, introduced in the April 2021 Update. It upgrades from the Swordsman (or its replacements) or the Gaesatae and requires Iron.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Man-At-Arms requires 20 Iron to train.
* Attributes:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
