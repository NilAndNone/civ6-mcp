## Summary

The Frigate is a Renaissance Era naval ranged unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Quadrireme (or its replacements).
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Frigate requires 20 Niter to train.
* Attributes:
** Has a ranged attack with Range 2.
