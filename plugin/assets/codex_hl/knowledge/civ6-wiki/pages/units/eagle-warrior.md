## Summary

Eagle warrior
The Eagle Warrior is a unique melee unit of the Aztec civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Warrior.
* Common traits:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
** Costs no Gold to maintain.
* Special abilities:
** Can capture defeated enemy units, turning them into Builders for the Aztecs.
* Special traits:
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (28 vs. 20).
** Higher Production cost (65 vs. 40).
