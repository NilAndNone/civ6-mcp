## Summary

Cossacks
The Cossack is a unique light cavalry unit of the Russian civilization in Civilization VI. It replaces the Cavalry.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Cossack requires 10 Horses to train.
* Common abilities:
** Ignores enemy zone of control.
* Special abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength when fighting in or next to home territory.
** Can move after attacking.
* Special traits:
** Higher Production cost (340 vs. 330).
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (67 vs. 62).
