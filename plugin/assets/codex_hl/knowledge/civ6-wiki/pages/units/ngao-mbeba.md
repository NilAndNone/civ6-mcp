## Summary

Shield bearer
The Ngao Mbeba is a unique melee unit of the Kongolese civilization. It replaces the Swordsman.
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Ngao Mbeba requires 5 Iron to train.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** Does not suffer Movement or sight penalties in Woods or Rainforests.
** +10 Strength Combat Strength when defending against ranged attacks.
* Special traits:
**Higher Strength Combat Strength (38 vs. 35).
**Higher Production cost (110 vs. 90).
