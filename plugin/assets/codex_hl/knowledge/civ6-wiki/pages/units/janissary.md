## Summary

Janissaries
The Janissary is a unique melee unit of the Ottoman civilization (when led by Suleiman (Kanuni)) in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. It replaces the Musketman and requires 10 Niter to train.
* Common abilities:
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
* Special abilities:
** Receives one free Promotion when first trained, purchased, or upgraded from an earlier unit.
** Can only be trained in cities with a Citizen Population of 2 or more.
** If trained in a city that was founded by the Ottomans, subtracts 1 from its Citizen Population.
* Special traits:
** Lower Production cost (120 vs. 240).
** Higher Strength Combat Strength (60 vs. 55).
