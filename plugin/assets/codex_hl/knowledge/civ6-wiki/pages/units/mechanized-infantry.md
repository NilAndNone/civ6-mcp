## Summary

The Mechanized Infantry is an Information Era melee unit in Civilization VI. It upgrades from the Infantry (or its replacements).
In the Gathering Storm expansion, the Mechanized Infantry requires 1 Oil to train and 1 Oil per turn to maintain.
* Attributes:
** Has 3 Moves.
** +5 Strength Combat Strength vs. anti-cavalry units.
