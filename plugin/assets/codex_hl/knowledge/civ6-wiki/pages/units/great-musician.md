## Summary

Musician
The Great Musician is a type of Great Person in Civilization VI dedicated to the creation of Music Great Works of Music. Each Great Musician may be activated up to three times on a tile containing an empty slot which accepts Great Works of Music (such as a Theater Square district with a Broadcast Center, a Palace, or suitable wonder), creating up to two Great Works of Music.

- movement: 4
